What the VIN tells you

The 2021 Trailblazer (1st-gen, GM VSS-S platform shared with the Buick Encore GX) offers two turbo three-cylinders, and the 8th VIN digit tells them apart: '2' = the 1.2L turbo (option LIH, 137 hp) and 'L' = the 1.3L turbo (option L3T, 155 hp). The engine code also implies the drivetrain and gearbox: the 1.2L is front-wheel-drive only and always pairs with a CVT, while the 1.3L pairs with a CVT on front-drive cars or a 9-speed automatic on AWD cars — so a Trailblazer that decodes to 'L' AND has AWD is the 9-speed car (verify the 9-speed fluid history), whereas any '2' or a front-drive 'L' is a CVT (verify CVT fluid service). Both are small turbocharged engines sensitive to oil-change intervals, so confirm 5,000-mile oil history regardless. Decode the full VIN at vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov to confirm engine and drivetrain, and run it at nhtsa.gov to check open recalls before purchase.
