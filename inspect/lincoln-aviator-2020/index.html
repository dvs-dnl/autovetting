2020 Lincoln Aviator
Standard / Reserve / Black Label / Grand Touring PHEV (2nd gen CD6, 2020–present)
Monitor

The Aviator is Lincoln's 3-row luxury SUV on the Explorer's CD6 platform. The 3.0T EcoBoost V6 is strong and well-matched. The Grand Touring PHEV adds 21 miles of EV range. Main issues: early production had InControl Touch Pro infotainment glitches and an air suspension compressor recall. The base active suspension can fail on higher-mileage units. Only buy with Lincoln CPO or complete dealer records.

Air Suspension

Air compressor failure (PerfectPosition)

High Risk at 60k+
Lincoln PerfectPosition air suspension compressor fails at similar rate to German rivals.
Overnight level test on any air-equipped Aviator.
Est. cost: $1,500–$3,000 Source: Lincoln Aviator air suspension pattern

Recall

Air suspension function (known weak point)

High Attention
The Aviator air suspension is a known trouble spot — air struts, lines, and the compressor can leak or fail, and repairs are expensive.
Confirm the vehicle sits level and raises/lowers correctly; listen for the compressor cycling excessively. Run the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls and confirm any open campaigns are closed.
Est. cost: $1,000-$3,000+ per corner if struts or compressor fail Source: AutoVetting editorial
