The 4th-gen Escape is a substantial redesign with available hybrid and PHEV options. The 1.5T 3-cylinder has documented oil leak issues at cylinder head/block junction on early builds — this was a significant warranty repair for Ford. The 2.0T is more reliable. PHEV buyers should verify all-electric range and battery health. 2022+ models have addressed most first-year concerns.

Engine

1.5T EcoBoost 3-cylinder — oil leak risk

High Risk on 2020-2021
Ford acknowledged a cylinder head crack/oil leak issue on 2020–2021 1.5T EcoBoost 3-cylinder engines. Some required engine replacement under warranty. Check for oil seeps at block-to-head junction.
Inspect block/head junction area for oil seeps or staining. Ask for any engine-related warranty work history.
Est. cost: Free dealer repair if in warranty; $3,000–$6,000 out-of-warranty Source: Ford 1.5T EcoBoost cylinder head issue / NHTSA investigation

2.0T EcoBoost — better choice

Low Risk
The 2.0T (SE Sport, Titanium) is the more reliable engine choice on this platform.
Standard oil and cold-start check. No widespread issues.
Est. cost: Routine maintenance Source: Ford 2.0T EcoBoost reliability
