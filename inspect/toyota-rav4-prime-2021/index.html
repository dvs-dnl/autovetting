2021 Toyota RAV4 Prime
SE / XSE / XSE Premium / XSE Technology (1st gen, 2021–present)
Low Risk

The RAV4 Prime combines plug-in hybrid efficiency with strong AWD performance — 302 hp system output, 42 miles all-electric range, and Toyota's proven THS-II hybrid architecture. One of the best PHEV SUVs available. No widespread failure modes. Main buyer checks: confirm PHEV charges and depletes normally, HV battery SoH, and verify any NHTSA recalls are closed. Outstanding value compared to pure EVs with comparable range.

HV battery charging check

THS-II hybrid system — proven

Low Risk
Toyota Hybrid System II is extremely reliable. Combined with PHEV battery for extra EV range.
Test EV mode, hybrid mode, and all-wheel drive engagement.

AWD System

Electric rear motor AWD

Low Risk
Rear axle driven by electric motor — no mechanical AWD coupling. Test by activating AWD-Lock in snowy conditions or on low-grip surfaces.
Confirm no AWD warning lights. Test low-grip surface performance if available.
