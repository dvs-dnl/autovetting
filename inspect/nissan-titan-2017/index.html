Low Risk

The 2nd-gen Titan with the 5.6L Endurance V8 is a reliable full-size truck that competes well against F-150 and Silverado on durability. No chronic powertrain failures. Main buyer checks are the transmission fluid service history, suspension wear on PRO-4X off-road models, and verifying the optional towing package if heavy towing is planned. Lower resale than domestic competitors makes it a value buy for work-truck buyers.

NHTSA Recalls (verify completion via VIN)

19V-495
Alternator Harness Chafe (gasoline Titan only)
2017-2019 gasoline, light-duty Titan - not the Cummins Titan XD: the alternator harness can be damaged during engine installation and short, stalling the engine, with an added fire risk. Dealer re-routes and clips the harness or replaces it. The 4th VIN digit tells you whether this campaign can apply to the truck in front of you.
18V-240
Missing Load-Capacity Modification Label
2016-2018 Titan (plus 2016 and 2018 Titan XD) delivered with factory accessories that cut load capacity, but without the modification label FMVSS 110 requires. The remedy is a label and instructions - no hardware - but the point for a buyer is that the door-jamb payload figure can overstate what the truck may legally carry.
17V-268
Rear Seat-Belt D-Ring Trim Cap (Crew Cab only)
2016-2017 Titan and Titan XD Crew Cab only: in a crash a rear occupant head can strike the seat-belt D-ring bolt trim cap - an FMVSS 201 failure. Dealer adds energy-absorbing material to the C-pillar finishers and replaces the bolt and cap. King Cab and Single Cab trucks are outside the scope, so this one depends on body style, not just year.

What the VIN tells you

Do not use the 8th VIN digit to identify this truck's engine — Nissan encodes the Titan/Titan XD powertrain in the 4th digit. 4th digit ‘A’ = 5.6L VK56VD Endurance gas V8; 4th digit ‘B’ = 5.0L Cummins V8 turbodiesel. The Cummins was offered only in the heavy-duty Titan XD, never in the half-ton Titan, so the 4th digit also tells you which truck you are actually looking at — Titan and Titan XD share a nameplate and a showroom but ride on different frames with different payload, brakes and pricing. The inspection diverges completely: a Cummins XD needs DEF and DPF regeneration history, fuel-filter service records (two filters, not one) and a scan for stored emissions codes; a VK56VD gas truck needs none of that and should instead be checked for 7-speed automatic fluid service history and, on PRO-4X, off-road suspension and skid-plate wear. Decode the full VIN at autovetting.com/decode to confirm engine and body before applying this checklist, and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
