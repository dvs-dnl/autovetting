2018 Hyundai Sonata
SE / SEL / Eco / Sport / Limited (LF, 7th gen, 2015-2019)
High Attention

The LF Sonata is a well-designed midsize sedan at a value price — but both the 2.4L Nu and 2.0T Theta II engines are in the Hyundai KSDS recall family for rod bearing wear. Multiple campaigns apply. This is the most important pre-purchase check. The 1.6T Eco is less common and somewhat lower risk. Sonata is a strong value when all recall campaigns are verified complete.

Powertrain

Nu / Theta II engine KSDS recall — critical

Critical
Multiple KSDS recall campaigns cover both Sonata engines. Bearing wear can lead to engine seizure.
Run VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls BEFORE any test drive. All campaigns must be complete.

Theta II 2.0T oil maintenance (Sport)

High Attention
Theta II requires synthetic oil every 5k miles. Extended intervals = accelerated bearing wear.
Check oil condition and confirm complete service records on any 2.0T.
Est. cost: $6,000–$9,000 engine Source: Hyundai 2.0T service requirement
