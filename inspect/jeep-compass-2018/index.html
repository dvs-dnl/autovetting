2018 Jeep Compass
Sport / Latitude / Limited / Trailhawk (MP, 2nd gen, 2017-present; MY2017 overlapped the outgoing MK)
Watchful

The MP-generation Compass (2017+) is a significant improvement over its predecessor — better quality, refined interior, and genuine trail capability on Trailhawk. The 2.4L Tigershark has the same oil consumption concern as in the Cherokee. The 9-speed ZF automatic needs careful testing. Value pricing makes it one of the more affordable new Jeep entry points on the used market.
