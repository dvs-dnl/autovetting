What the VIN tells you

The 2024 CX-90 splits across two very different powertrains, and confirming which one you are looking at is the key decode. Most cars use the 3.3L e-Skyactiv-G turbocharged inline-six (280 hp on Select/Preferred, 340 hp on Carbon Edition and above with premium fuel) backed by a 48V mild-hybrid system and an 8-speed automatic. The alternative is the e-Skyactiv PHEV, a 2.5L four-cylinder plus electric motor (323 hp combined, about 26 miles of EV range). The two share almost no inspection priorities: PHEV buyers must verify charging, the high-voltage battery, and EV-mode engagement, while inline-six buyers should confirm the 48V mild-hybrid starter/alternator behaves on restart. Mazda's VIN encodes the powertrain — run the full VIN through NHTSA vPIC at vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov to confirm inline-six vs PHEV before applying this checklist. Position 10 encodes model year: R = 2024.
