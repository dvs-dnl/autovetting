What the VIN tells you

The 7th-gen ES (XZ10, 2019–2025) launched with two distinct powertrains and the inspection priorities are completely different. ES 350 — 3.5L 2GR-FKS V6 (302hp) with 8-speed automatic, FWD. This is one of Toyota's most-proven port-and-direct injection V6s; primary concern is GDI carbon buildup at 80k+ miles (manageable with walnut blast). ES 300h — 2.5L A25A-FXS Atkinson-cycle inline-4 paired with Toyota's hybrid synergy drive system (215hp combined). The hybrid drivetrain has no documented chronic issues at the 6-year point, but the hybrid battery cooling fan filter (cabin air filter — the hybrid battery uses cabin air for cooling) is a service item generic mechanics often miss. The hybrid loses ~$3–4k in resale vs the V6 at the same trim, so a hybrid at V6 money is a strong buy. Easy ID: badge on the trunk lid. Full VIN decode at autovetting.com/inspect.
