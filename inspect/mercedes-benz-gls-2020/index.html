The X167 GLS is Mercedes flagship 3-row luxury SUV — a genuine S-Class on stilts with optional E-Active Body Control suspension. Air suspension compressor failure is the primary mechanical concern. The M256 I6 with 48V EQ Boost is smooth and efficient; the GLS 580 V8 is powerful but expensive to maintain. Only buy with full Mercedes dealer records and CPO coverage.

Air Suspension

Air suspension compressor failure

High Risk at 50k+
GLS Airmatic compressor fails at similar rate to GLE. Very expensive on this platform.
Overnight level test. Any corner sag = compressor failing.
Est. cost: $2,000–$4,000 compressor Source: Mercedes X167 air suspension pattern

Powertrain

M256 I6 EQ Boost — preferred

Low Risk
Inline-six with 48V mild hybrid is the better long-term ownership choice.
Standard oil check. Test all driving modes.
Est. cost: Higher maintenance cost Source: Mercedes M256 reliability

GLS 580 V8 — powerful but expensive

Monitor

Full oil check and cold-start test.
