7-speed DCT shudder (1.6T)

Monitor
The 7-speed dual-clutch transmission on 1.6T Konas can shudder at 15–25 mph during light throttle engagement. Hyundai issued software updates that resolve most cases.
Test slow crawl in traffic at 15–25 mph. Any shudder = request dealer DCT software update (free).

NHTSA Recalls (verify completion via VIN)

21V-301
2.0L Nu MPI piston oil rings not properly heat-treated — engine damage, stall and fire risk
2019-2021 Kona, 2019-2020 Elantra and 2019-2021 Veloster built with the 2.0L Nu MPI engine, 158,493 units. Piston oil rings that were not properly heat-treated can damage the engine and stall it, and oil leaking onto hot exhaust parts can start a fire. Dealers inspect and replace the engine at no charge and deploy Piston Ring Noise Sensing System (PNSS) software; owner letters were mailed 2021-08-24. This is the engine-code item on this page: the campaign covers the 2.0L only, so an SE or SEL is in scope and a 1.6T Limited or Ultimate is not. The trim badge narrows it and the VIN settles it.

What the VIN tells you

The 1st-gen Kona (OS, 2018-2023) came with two completely different gas engines AND an electric variant (Kona EV — separate checklist). 2.0L MPI Nu I4 (147hp), naturally aspirated, paired with 6-speed automatic — fitted to SE and SEL trims. 1.6L Gamma Turbo GDI I4 (175hp), paired with 7-speed dual-clutch automatic — fitted to Limited and Ultimate trims. Critical disambiguation: the 2.0L Nu MPI is the engine named in NHTSA campaign 21V-301, where improperly heat-treated piston oil rings can damage the engine, and Hyundai's remedy pairs an engine inspection or replacement with Piston Ring Noise Sensing System (PNSS) software; the 1.6T uses a different (Gamma) family and is not in that campaign but has its own carbon-buildup and timing-chain stretch concerns above 80k miles. Practical buyer rule: verify ALL open Hyundai engine recalls are closed via VIN at the dealer or hyundaiusa.com regardless of engine — the 2.0L exposure is the bigger concern. The 7-speed dual-clutch on the 1.6T needs trans fluid service every 30k miles. Tell engines apart by the trim badge: SE/SEL = 2.0L NA, Limited/Ultimate = 1.6T.
