2019 Kia Forte
FE / LXS / GT-Line / EX / GT (BD, 3rd gen, 2019-2024)
Watchful

The BD Forte is Kia's best compact sedan — well-styled, feature-rich, and priced aggressively. The 2.0L Nu engine carries KSDS recall exposure (same family as Sonata). Verify all recall campaigns before purchase. The 1.6T GT is genuinely fun. IVT/CVT fluid service is the critical maintenance document on the base 2.0L. Strong value when recalls are confirmed complete.
