What the VIN tells you

The 2nd-gen Venza (AX10, 2021+) is hybrid-only: every car uses the 2.5L A25A-FXS hybrid system (219 hp combined) with electronic on-demand e-AWD standard across LE, XLE, and Limited, so there is no engine or drivetrain fork — the 8th VIN digit encodes that single restraint/engine combination only. The decode that matters is generation: the 10th-digit model year (M = 2021) confirms this Harrier-based hybrid crossover and separates it from the unrelated 1st-gen Venza (2009-2015), a Camry-based wagon offered with a gas 2.7L four or 3.5L 2GR-FE V6 and conventional FWD/AWD. Trim (LE/XLE/Limited) changes only features such as the Limited's electrochromic Star Gaze glass roof, not the powertrain. Confirm year and trim at vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov.
