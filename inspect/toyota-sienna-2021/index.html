What the VIN tells you

The 5th-generation Sienna (XL50, 2021-present) is hybrid-only in the U.S., so there is no engine fork to decode — every 2021 Sienna uses the same 2.5L A25A-FXS four-cylinder (245 hp combined) with an electronic CVT, and the 8th VIN digit encodes only that single hybrid combination. The decode that actually matters is the 10th VIN digit (model year): 'M' = 2021, confirming the new XL50 hybrid rather than the unrelated 4th-gen Sienna (2011-2020), which used a 3.5L 2GR V6 gas engine with a conventional automatic and shares neither parts nor inspection priorities. The only real configuration variant on a 2021 is drivetrain — front-wheel drive or available electric-rear-motor AWD — so confirm whether AWD is present (check for the rear electric coupling and its 30k-mile fluid service). Early A25A-FXS cars (2021) had reports of EGR-system issues, so verify no related warning lights. Decode the full VIN at vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov and run it at nhtsa.gov for open recalls before purchase.
