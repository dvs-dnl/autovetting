What the VIN tells you

Single engine — the US 5th-gen (N280) 4Runner offered only the 4.0L 1GR-FE V6 (270hp) with a 5-speed automatic, so nothing on the powertrain changes by VIN. Toyota encodes the engine in the 5th VIN character (U = 1GR-FE), not the 8th; the 8th digit identifies the model line. The spec that actually varies is the drivetrain — SR5 and TRD Off-Road run part-time 4WD, the Limited runs full-time 4WD with a lockable center differential, and SR5/Limited were also sold in rear-drive 2WD. Confirm which one the specific truck is before you negotiate.
