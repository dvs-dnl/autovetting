2018 Ford Expedition

Watchful

The 4th-gen Expedition is Ford's full-size 3-row BOF SUV — aluminium body panels over a steel frame, twin-turbo EcoBoost V6. The 3.5L EcoBoost is proven in F-150 applications; the main buyer checks are cam phaser noise on cold start and 10-speed transmission shudder. MAX wheelbase adds genuine third-row legroom. Strong full-size SUV value versus GM competition.

Top concern
3.5T EcoBoost cam phasers / 10-speed transmission shudder

Powertrain

3.5L EcoBoost cam phaser noise

Monitor
Cam phaser rattle on cold start is reported on 3.5T EcoBoost above 60k miles — same issue as F-150 EcoBoost.
Cold-start and listen for rattle in first 5 seconds.
Est. cost: $1,200–$3,000 cam phaser replacement Source: Ford 3.5T EcoBoost pattern

10-speed 10R80 transmission shudder

High Attention
The 10R80 has TSBs for shudder at 25–45 mph under light throttle — same as F-150.
Test extensively at light throttle 25–45 mph for shudder.
Est. cost: $350–$550 fluid + calibration / $4,500+ transmission Source: Ford TSB 19-2200

Frame & Body

Aluminium body panel repair cost awareness

Monitor
Aluminium panels are rust-resistant but expensive to repair after collision. Any accident history = higher repair costs.
Run CARFAX. Inspect all body panels for repair or replacement evidence.
Est. cost: Aluminium repair typically 30–50% more than steel Source: Insurance industry data

Electrical

SYNC 3 and Ford Co-Pilot360 features

Low Risk
SYNC 3 is reliable. Test all driver assist features.
Test CarPlay, backup camera, and all safety system indicators.
Est. cost: $0 update Source: Ford campaigns

Third Row

Power fold third-row operation

Low Risk
Power-fold third row can bind at 80k+ miles.
Cycle third row through all positions.
Est. cost: $200–$500 mechanism Source: Common wear

Documentation

Recall check + transmission TSB history

High Attention
Four recalls possible. 10-speed TSB documentation is important.
Run nhtsa.gov/recalls. Confirm transmission software update.
Est. cost: Walk away from unresolved safety recalls Source: NHTSA
