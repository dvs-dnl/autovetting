2018 Toyota Land Cruiser
Base (J200, 2008–2021)
Low Risk

The J200 Land Cruiser is one of the most durable vehicles ever made — legendary off-road capability, ultra-reliable drivetrain, and Toyota build quality. They commonly reach 300k–400k miles with routine maintenance. The 5.7L 3UR-FE is the same engine as the Sequoia. The Land Cruiser is not on Toyota's Takata affected-model list, but a VIN recall check is still mandatory. Frame rust in northern states is worth checking. High purchase price reflects the high residual value.

Recall

Open recalls — check VIN (the J200 is not a Takata vehicle)

Monitor
The Land Cruiser does not appear on Toyota's Takata affected-model list. Its Lexus cousin the GX460 does — which is probably where the claim came from — but the GX is a different platform (150-series) with different restraint hardware, and one does not imply the other. Run the VIN anyway: the J200 has real campaigns.
Run the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls before you go and see the car. Any campaign that comes back open = do not purchase until the dealer has closed it. Recall work is always free, and completion is logged against the VIN, so a seller's word is not evidence.
Est. cost: Free under recall Source: Toyota Takata affected-model list (toyota.com/recall/takata), verified 2026-08-09

Low Risk
