2018 Volkswagen Tiguan
S / SE / SEL / SEL Premium R-Line (MQB, 2nd gen, 2018-2024)
Watchful

The 2nd-gen Tiguan (2018+) on the MQB platform is a substantial improvement over the 1st gen. The EA888 2.0T is the same family as Audi's engine but with different tuning. GDI carbon buildup on intake valves is a long-term service item. DSG fluid service is required regularly. Strong compact SUV value for buyers who maintain the service schedule.

Powertrain

EA888 timing chain tensioner

Monitor
Same EA888 timing chain tensioner concern as Audi A4. Cold-start rattle = tensioner wear.
Cold-start rattle check first 5 seconds.
Est. cost: $1,200–$2,500 timing chain service Source: EA888 pattern

DSG fluid service (if DSG-equipped)

High Attention
The DSG (DQ381 8-speed wet-clutch) on 2nd-gen Tiguan shudders at low speed when fluid is overdue.
Test stop-and-go under 15 mph.
Est. cost: $400–$600 DSG fluid / $2,000–$3,500 clutch pack Source: VW DSG maintenance standard

AWD

4MOTION coupling fluid

Monitor
Haldex AWD coupling requires annual fluid service. Vibration at highway speed = neglected.
Test highway cornering.
Est. cost: $150–$300 Haldex fluid service Source: Haldex AWD standard

Electrical

MIB2 infotainment

Low Risk
MIB2 is reliable. Known CarPlay drop issues resolved by software update.
Test all infotainment functions.
Est. cost: $0 update Source: VW campaigns

Body

Panoramic roof drain

Monitor
Same panoramic drain clog issue as other VW group vehicles.
Check A-pillar headliner for water stains.
Est. cost: $100–$300 drain cleaning Source: VW group common issue

Documentation

DSG service history + recall check

High Attention
DSG fluid history is critical. Three recalls possible.
Request DSG service receipt. Run nhtsa.gov/recalls.
Est. cost: Walk away from DSG without service history Source: NHTSA
