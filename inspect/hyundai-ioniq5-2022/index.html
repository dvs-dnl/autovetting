The IONIQ 5 on the E-GMP platform is one of the best EVs launched since the Tesla Model 3 — 800V architecture enables ultra-fast charging (18 min to 80% at 350kW DCFC), distinctive retro styling, and a flat floor that maximizes interior space. Battery & Charging

HV battery health and range

High Attention
IONIQ 5 battery should show minimal degradation under 40k miles. Compare current range to EPA rating (266 mi LR RWD, 266 mi LR AWD).
Charge to 80% and verify range estimate matches EPA. More than 10% loss = investigate.
Est. cost: $8,000–$16,000 HV battery if degraded Source: E-GMP battery data

NHTSA Recalls (verify completion via VIN)

22V-324
Shifter Software Rollaway
SCU software error can disengage the parking mechanism and allow rollaway — 2022 Ioniq 5, campaign 228. Use the electronic parking brake until the dealer updates the software.
24V-868
ICCU 12V Charging Failure
Integrated Charging Control Unit can fail and stop charging the 12V battery — loss of drive power; 2022-2024 Ioniq 5, campaign 272. Replaces 24V-204: cars fixed under the old remedy need the new one. Dealer inspects/replaces the ICCU and fuse and updates software.
Multiple
Open recalls — VIN check
Campaigns vary by build date. Run the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls and require every open recall closed before purchase.

What the VIN tells you

IONIQ 5 (E-GMP) is electric-only, so the 8th VIN digit encodes the motor/battery configuration rather than a combustion engine, reported as B for single-motor RWD and C for dual-motor AWD (320 hp, 77.4 kWh). Treat the B/C split as confirm-at-vpic (single-source) and verify motor count plus heat-pump fitment from the build sheet, since the heat pump is standard on AWD but optional on RWD. 10th digit N = 2022 (P = 2023).
