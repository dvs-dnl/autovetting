High Attention

2nd-gen XC60 on the SPA platform is the XC90's smaller sibling — shared platform means shared issues. Sensus infotainment reliability is the most common real-world complaint. Drive-E timing belt service required at 100k miles. Air suspension (if equipped) and T8 battery health are additional considerations. Volvo specialist PPI with VIDA scan is mandatory.

Powertrain

Drive-E timing belt service

High Attention
T5 and T6 Drive-E engines use a rubber timing belt requiring replacement at 100k miles or 10 years.
Request timing belt service record on any T5/T6 with 80k+ miles.
Est. cost: $800–$1,500 timing belt service / $6,000–$9,000 engine if belt breaks Source: Volvo Drive-E service schedule

T8 PHEV battery health

Monitor
T8 PHEV battery should provide 17+ miles EV range (18.8 kWh usable).
Charge to full, test EV range.
Est. cost: $6,000–$12,000 HV battery Source: T8 wear pattern

Electrical

Sensus infotainment freeze and reboot

High Attention
Sensus on SPA XC60 freezes, reboots, and loses GPS — same pattern as XC90.
Test all functions extensively during inspection.
Est. cost: $0 update / $1,500–$3,500 head unit Source: Volvo Sensus complaints

Suspension

Air suspension (if equipped)

High Attention
Air spring and compressor wear at 80k–120k miles.
Check ride height level. Listen for compressor cycling.
Est. cost: $600–$1,200 per spring / $800–$1,500 compressor Source: SPA air suspension pattern

AWD

Haldex AWD coupling annual service

Monitor
Annual Haldex fluid service required.
Test highway cornering for vibration.
Est. cost: $150–$300 Haldex service Source: Haldex standard

Documentation

Critical
