Powertrain

AFM lifter failure — mandatory check

Critical
All Yukon V8s use AFM. Lifters fail at 60k–100k miles on oil neglect.
Cold-start ticking check + P0300 scan + oil change records.
Est. cost: $3,500–$7,000 lifter replacement Source: K2XX AFM pattern

6L80 transmission fluid

Monitor
Light-throttle shudder at 30–45 mph = fluid contamination.
Test at highway light throttle.
Est. cost: $350–$550 flush Source: 6L80 service pattern

Rear air leveling system

Monitor

Verify rear sits level. Listen for compressor cycling at idle.
Est. cost: $400–$900 per bag / $600–$1,200 compressor Source: Common K2XX wear
