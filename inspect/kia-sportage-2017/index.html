2017 Kia Sportage
LX / EX / SX Turbo / SX Limited (QL, 4th gen, 2017-2022)
Watchful

The QL Sportage is a solid compact SUV — good value retention and a comfortable ride. The 2.4L Nu engine is subject to Kia's KSDS recall campaign for rod bearing wear. Verified recall completion is the single most important pre-purchase step. The 2.0T SX is the performance option but requires strict oil intervals.

Top concern
Engine KSDS recall / bearing wear

Powertrain

Nu engine bearing recall (KSDS)

High Attention
Rod bearing wear can lead to engine seizure. Multiple KSDS campaigns apply to Nu I4 engines.
Run VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. All engine bearing campaigns must be complete. Listen for metallic knock at warm idle.
Est. cost: $0 under recall / $5,500–$8,000 engine Source: Kia KSDS recall family

2.0T turbo oil maintenance (SX)

Monitor
SX turbo requires full-synthetic oil every 5k miles. Extended intervals lead to turbo coking.
Check oil condition + confirm service record on any SX.
Est. cost: $800–$2,200 turbo repair Source: 2.0T GDI maintenance standard

DCT / 6AT fluid

Monitor
Low-speed shudder on DCT trims indicates clutch wear or fluid degradation.
Test stop-and-go driving under 15 mph.
Est. cost: $250–$450 fluid service Source: Kia DCT pattern

AWD System

Rear coupling fluid service

Monitor
AWD coupling fluid required every 30–45k. Neglect causes vibration and whine at highway speed.
Test highway cornering for vibration or pull.
Est. cost: $150–$300 service / $800–$1,500 coupling Source: AWD maintenance requirement

Electrical

UVO infotainment

Low Risk
UVO connectivity issues are common; software updates resolve most.
Test CarPlay/Android Auto and backup camera.
Est. cost: $0 update Source: Kia campaigns

Documentation

Engine recall complete — mandatory check

Critical
KSDS campaigns are non-negotiable. Service history proves oil discipline on turbo models.
Run VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. Walk away if engine campaigns are open.
Est. cost: Walk away from unresolved engine recalls Source: NHTSA
