2018 Ford Mustang
EcoBoost / GT / GT Premium / Bullitt / GT350 (S550, 2015-2023)
Watchful

The S550 Mustang is one of the most satisfying performance cars available used. The 5.0L Coyote V8 is a proven workhorse; EcoBoost 4-cylinder is the fuel-efficient daily driver. Main buyer risk: assess track use and abuse on any GT, GT Premium, or GT350. Cam phaser noise is reported on EcoBoost cars above 60k miles. MT82 manual transmission has known notchy shift quality that worsened with use.

Powertrain

EcoBoost cam phaser noise

Monitor
Same 2.3T EcoBoost cam phaser rattle as Ranger — reported above 60k miles.
Cold-start listen for rattle in first 5 seconds.
Est. cost: $800–$2,500 cam phaser replacement Source: Ford 2.3T pattern

5.0L Coyote — healthy engine check

Low Risk
The 5.0L Coyote is highly robust. Standard pre-purchase checks apply.
Cold-start, oil check, standard inspection.
Est. cost: Maintenance only Source: Coyote reliability record

Performance/Abuse

Track use and abuse assessment

High Attention
GT350 and GT Performance Pack cars are frequently tracked. Clutch, brakes, tires, and differential wear dramatically on track use.
Inspect brake pad thickness, tire wear pattern (inner vs. outer), and listen for differential whine.
Est. cost: $1,500–$4,000 performance brake and clutch service Source: Performance car inspection standard

MT82 manual transmission shift quality

Monitor
MT82 is notoriously notchy when cold — this is a known characteristic, not always a defect.
Test all gears cold and warm. Any grinding or difficulty engaging 3rd = synchro wear.
Est. cost: $200–$400 fluid service (helps) / $1,500–$3,000 synchro rebuild Source: MT82 known characteristic

Suspension

MagneRide or standard shock wear

Monitor
MagneRide shocks fail when driven hard. Standard shocks wear normally.
Drive over rough pavement — harsh impacts or bottoming = shock wear.
Est. cost: $500–$1,500 per corner (MagneRide is expensive) Source: Mustang suspension pattern

Electrical

SYNC 3 infotainment

Low Risk
SYNC 3 is reliable. Test all functions.
Test CarPlay, backup camera, heated/cooled seat controls.
Est. cost: $0 update Source: Ford campaigns

Documentation

Track history + recall check

High Attention
Track use is the critical variable on any GT or GT350.
Run nhtsa.gov/recalls. Walk away from undocumented track-use cars without price adjustment.
Est. cost: Walk away from high-track GT350 without specialist PPI Source: NHTSA

NHTSA Recalls (verify completion via VIN)

17V-814
Instrument Cluster PRNDL — Rollaway
Automatic cars with the 12.4-inch digital cluster may not display or chime the gear position at key-off out of Park — rollaway risk. Small population (~2,100); dealer updates cluster software.
18V-213
10-Speed Auto — Missing Park Roll Pin
A missing transmission roll pin can cause loss of the Park function. Tiny population (142 vehicles with the 10R80). Dealer inspects and repairs.
Multiple
Open recalls — VIN check
Campaigns vary by build date. Run the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls and require every open recall closed before purchase.
