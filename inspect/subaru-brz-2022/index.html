What the VIN tells you

Every 2nd-gen BRZ (ZD8, 2022+) uses the same engine — the 2.4L FA24 naturally-aspirated boxer four (228 hp) — so there is no engine-family ambiguity to decode, and on Subarus the 8th VIN digit encodes the restraint/airbag system rather than an engine letter anyway. The fork that actually matters on a used BRZ is the transmission: a 6-speed manual or a 6-speed Aisin automatic. The manual is the enthusiast's choice and the one most likely to have been tracked — check the clutch engagement point and look for autocross tire and brake wear; the automatic is smoother for commuting and typically less abused. The FA24 also retired the FA20's head-gasket reputation, so coolant condition is a routine check rather than a red flag. The most useful VIN steps here are confirming model year at the 10th digit (N = 2022, P = 2023) and running the full VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
