What the VIN tells you

The 1st-gen Stinger (CK, 2018–2023) is sold with two turbocharged engines that carry different inspection priorities, and trim is the quickest tell: the GT-Line uses the 2.5L Smartstream turbo four (300 hp), while GT1 and GT2 use the 3.3L Lambda II twin-turbo V6 (368 hp) — the enthusiast's choice. Both drive an 8-speed torque-converter automatic; the Stinger has never used a dual-clutch gearbox, so any 'DCT' description you see is incorrect. On the 3.3T, confirm both turbos spool cleanly from cold and check for spirited-use brake and tire wear; the 2.5T is the simpler, lower-cost-to-own choice. In Kia VINs the 8th digit encodes the engine, but confirm the engine for your specific VIN at autovetting.com/decode or vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov rather than relying on the letter alone. Run the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls before purchase.
