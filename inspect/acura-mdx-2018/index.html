V6 VCM right-rear motor mount wear

What the VIN tells you

The 3rd-gen MDX (YD3, 2014–2020) came with two completely different powertrains in 2018, and the inspection priorities differ significantly between them. 3.5L J35Y V6 (290hp, 9-speed automatic, FWD or SH-AWD) is the mainstream engine — fitted to Standard / Technology / Advance / A-Spec trims. Known issues: the ZF 9HP 9-speed transmission had documented shift-quality issues from 2014–2016 that improved meaningfully by 2018, and the SH-AWD rear differential needs fluid service every 30k miles (not on Acura's stock interval). Sport Hybrid (Advance trim only, ~5% of 2018 production): 3.0L J30A5 V6 paired with three electric motors and a 7-speed DCT, total system output 321hp. The hybrid has its own concerns — the lithium-ion battery pack, the electric Super-Handling AWD rear motors, and DCT clutch wear are all separate inspection priorities not relevant to the V6. Tell them apart fastest by the badge on the tailgate ("Sport Hybrid" is explicit). Full VIN decode at autovetting.com/inspect.
