FB20 oil consumption (80k+ miles)

Monitor
FB20 can consume up to 1 qt per 1,000 miles above 80k miles with extended intervals.

What the VIN tells you

One engine for 2018: the direct-injection FB20D boxer-4 (152 hp), so the 8th VIN digit does not fork the engine — on these FB-family Subarus it encodes the emissions market, not the powertrain. The Crosstrek Hybrid (FB20 + a Toyota-sourced plug-in system, 148 hp combined) did not arrive until the 2019 model year and was a CARB-state compliance car, so a 2018 is gas-only — ignore any 'hybrid/PHEV' claim on a 2018 VIN. The real decode is the transmission: 2.0i base and Premium came standard with a 6-speed manual (Lineartronic CVT optional); Limited was CVT-only.
