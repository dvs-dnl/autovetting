2018 Cadillac Escalade
Luxury / Premium / Platinum (K2XX body-on-frame, 2015-2020)
High Attention

The K2XX Escalade uses exclusively the 6.2L V8 with AFM — the most expensive AFM failure scenario of any K2XX vehicle. Lifter failure at 60k–100k miles on neglected service costs $5,000–$8,000+. Every used Escalade buyer must verify AFM status. Magnetic Ride Control (MRC) suspension is a significant additional cost when dampers fail. Budget $6,000–$10,000 for deferred repairs on any high-mileage example.

Powertrain

6.2L AFM lifter failure — critical

Critical
All K2XX Escalades use the 6.2L L86 with AFM. Premium fuel + AFM + heavy vehicle = accelerated failure at 60k–100k miles with extended oil intervals.
Cold-start ticking + P0300 scan + oil interval records. Walk away if no records.
Est. cost: $5,000–$8,000 lifter replacement Source: K2XX 6.2L AFM pattern

10-speed 10L90 transmission

Monitor
2017+ Escalade uses the 10-speed, which has shudder TSBs. Pre-2017 uses 6L80.
Test light throttle 30–50 mph for shudder.
Est. cost: $400–$600 fluid + calibration Source: 10L90 service pattern

Suspension

Magnetic Ride Control damper failure

High Attention
MRC adaptive dampers fail at 80k–100k miles. Replacement is expensive.
Drive over rough pavement — harsh ride or clunking = failed MRC dampers.
Est. cost: $800–$1,500 per corner for MRC dampers Source: MRC failure pattern

Air ride level control (rear)

Monitor
Rear air leveling compressor and airbags wear at 100k+ miles.
Verify truck sits level side-to-side.
Est. cost: $400–$900 per bag / $600–$1,200 compressor Source: Common K2XX wear

Electronics

CUE infotainment system reliability

High Attention
Cadillac CUE touch-sensitive buttons and screen delaminate and fail. A well-known defect on 2015–2018 Escalades.
Test all CUE touch controls — any ghost touches, dead zones, or delamination = CUE failure.
Est. cost: $1,200–$2,500 CUE replacement Source: Escalade CUE known defect

Frame

Frame and undercarriage inspection

High Attention
Luxury BOF SUV + salt-belt = expensive frame rust.
Full undercarriage inspection.
Est. cost: Walk away from through-rust Source: Truck standard

Documentation

AFM history + MRC + CUE audit — all critical

Critical
AFM, MRC, and CUE are all significant expense items on an Escalade. All require documentation.
Run nhtsa.gov/recalls. Require full service history. Have a specialist inspect MRC dampers and scan CUE.
Est. cost: Budget $6,000–$10,000 deferred repair potential on unknown-history Escalades Source: NHTSA
