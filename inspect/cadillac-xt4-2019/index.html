2019 Cadillac XT4
Luxury / Premium Luxury / Sport (1st gen, 2019–present)
Monitor

The XT4 is Cadillac's entry luxury crossover — well-priced, well-equipped, and Cadillac's first compact luxury SUV. The 2.0T LTG has documented oil consumption, the main buyer concern. The 9-speed automatic is smooth. Cadillac CUE infotainment on early models was criticized for slow response. Competitive with Audi Q3 and BMW X1 at a lower price.
