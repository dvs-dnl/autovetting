What the VIN tells you

The 2nd-gen GR86 (ZN8, 2022–present) is built by Subaru alongside the BRZ and uses a single engine — the 2.4L FA24 naturally-aspirated boxer four (228 hp) — so there is no engine-family fork to decode; every U.S. GR86 has the same motor. (The GR86 carries a Subaru-style VIN in which the 8th digit encodes the restraint system, not the engine.) The distinction that actually changes your inspection is the transmission: a 6-speed manual or a 6-speed automatic. On manual cars, verify clutch engagement height (the middle third of pedal travel) and ask directly about track or autocross use; automatics have no clutch-wear concern but should have documented fluid history. Use the 10th VIN digit for model year (N = 2022, P = 2023). Confirm the transmission and exact spec with a full decode at autovetting.com/decode or vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov, and run the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls before purchase.
