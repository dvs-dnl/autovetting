2019 Kia Niro

Low Risk

The DE Niro is Kia's most fuel-efficient crossover — available as Hybrid (52 mpg combined), PHEV, or full EV. The hybrid drivetrain is shared with Hyundai Ioniq. Main buyer checks depend on variant: Hybrid — 6-speed DCT fluid service; PHEV — plug-in battery health; EV — HV battery health (64 kWh should provide 220+ miles). Outstanding fuel economy buy in any form.

Powertrain (Hybrid/PHEV)

6-speed DCT fluid service (Hybrid)

High Attention
The Niro Hybrid uses a 6-speed DCT. Shudder at low speed = overdue fluid.
Test stop-and-go under 15 mph. Request DCT fluid service receipt.
Est. cost: $250–$450 service / $3,500–$5,500 clutch pack Source: Kia DCT service standard

PHEV / EV battery health

High Attention
PHEV: test EV range (26 mi EPA). EV: should show 200+ miles on 90% charge.
Charge to 90% and verify range estimate.
Est. cost: $4,000–$10,000 battery depending on variant Source: Kia battery data
