The XT5 is Cadillac's mid-size luxury crossover replacement for the SRX. The 3.6L LGX V6 is proven and reliable. The main buyer concerns are the timing chain on the LGX (which is robust but fails if oil is neglected at high mileage), oil cooler seal leaks, and infotainment (CUE system on early models is slow and the touchpad wears out). 2019+ CUE updates improved the interface significantly.

CUE Infotainment

CUE touchscreen delamination / unresponsive

High Risk on 2017–2018
The Cadillac User Experience (CUE) system on 2017–2018 models uses a capacitive touchscreen that can delaminate or become unresponsive. This is a known defect Cadillac addressed under extended warranty on some VINs.
Test all touchscreen areas including corners. Any dead zones or sticky feel = replacement needed.
Est. cost: $900–$1,500 touchscreen replacement Source: Cadillac CUE TSB / owner pattern

NHTSA Recalls (verify completion via VIN)

16V-802
Front brake caliper piston seal torn or misaligned — brake fluid leak
2017 XT5 and 2017 GMC Acadia, 1,844 units. A torn or misaligned front brake caliper piston seal can let brake fluid leak past it and reduce braking performance. Dealers inspect the front calipers and replace the assemblies as needed at no charge; GM calls this recall 16096 and it began 2016-12-12. 1,844 units is a very narrow population for a model year, so the model year tells you almost nothing here — only the VIN does. Because the campaign is nearly ten years old, what you are checking is whether the completion was recorded, not whether the car is new enough to have escaped it.

What the VIN tells you

8th VIN digit engine code: 'S' = 3.6L LGX V6 (RPO LGX, ~310 hp, 8-speed automatic) — the only engine offered in the 2017 XT5, so the 8th digit simply confirms the LGX rather than selecting between engines. Do not confuse the XT5's LGX with the older SRX it replaced, which used the LF1/LFX 3.6L; a genuine XT5 VIN decodes to the LGX (8th digit S). The LGX runs Active Fuel Management (cylinder deactivation), so AFM lifter longevity and the timing chain both depend on clean oil — verify oil-change history. Run the full VIN at nhtsa.gov to confirm any open campaigns are closed before purchase.
