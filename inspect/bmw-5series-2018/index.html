2018 BMW 5 Series
530i / 540i / M550i / 530e PHEV (G30, 2017-2023)
High Attention

The G30 5 Series is BMW's executive sedan flagship — refined, powerful, and technologically sophisticated. Ownership costs are the highest of any BMW outside M cars. The B58 I6 is excellent; the N63 V8 has documented rod bearing and carbon buildup concerns. Electric water pump, control arm wear, and iDrive module faults are all real line items. BMW specialist PPI with ISTA scan is non-negotiable.

Powertrain

N63 V8 — rod bearing and carbon (M550i / 550i)

Critical
The N63 biturbo V8 has documented rod bearing concerns and significant carbon buildup on intake valves. Avoid unless confirmed CPO or with full service history. B58 I6 is significantly more reliable.
If buying N63 V8: require full oil change history, scan for fault codes, and get a specialist PPI.
Est. cost: $6,000–$12,000 rod bearing + carbon service if neglected Source: BMW N63 known pattern

B48 / B58 electric water pump and thermostat

High Attention
Electric water pump failure at 80k–120k miles on all B-series engines.
Check coolant level and expansion tank condition.
Est. cost: $600–$1,200 water pump + thermostat Source: BMW B-series water pump pattern

Chassis

Front and rear control arm bushing wear

High Attention
G30 control arms and bushings wear at 60k–80k miles.
Highway tracking test + PPI front suspension inspection.
Est. cost: $1,000–$2,500 suspension refresh Source: G30 common repair

Electronics

iDrive 6 faults and module errors

Monitor
G30 iDrive 6 is generally reliable but accumulates module fault codes.
PPI with ISTA diagnostic scan.
Est. cost: $200–$3,000+ depending on fault Source: BMW pre-purchase scan standard

Transmission

ZF 8HP fluid service

Monitor
"Lifetime" ZF fluid but independent shops recommend change at 60k.
Test for shudder at 25–45 mph.
Est. cost: $400–$600 fluid service Source: BMW shop consensus

Documentation

BMW specialist PPI — mandatory on any G30

Critical
G30 complexity means hidden costs without a specialist scan.
$150–$250 PPI with ISTA. Walk away from any seller who refuses.
Est. cost: $150–$250 PPI Source: BMW ownership standard
