Sahara 4xe / Rubicon 4xe (JL PHEV, 2021–present)
Monitor

The Wrangler 4xe is the plug-in hybrid JL — 22 miles all-electric range in a capable off-roader. The hybrid system adds significant weight and complexity. Early production had multiple electrical recalls. The PHEV battery adds to the Wrangler's already-high purchase and maintenance costs. Strong buy for buyers who want off-road capability plus commute-friendly EV range, but verify all recalls are closed — there were many on early 4xe builds.

Recalls — Check First

Multiple 4xe PHEV recalls (2021)

Critical — Run VIN
Early 4xe had recalls including HV battery management, powertrain software, and thermal management issues.
Run VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. All must be confirmed closed — there were 5+ early production recalls.
Est. cost: Free dealer repair Source: NHTSA Jeep 4xe recalls

PHEV System

HV battery SoH and charging

Monitor
17 kWh battery provides 22 miles EV range. Check it charges to full and depletes normally.
Verify PHEV charges normally and EV mode engages. No HV warning lights.
