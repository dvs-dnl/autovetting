Luxury / Premium Luxury / Sport / V (1st gen, 2020–present)
Low Risk

The CT5 replaces both the ATS and CTS — well-sorted rear-wheel-drive luxury sedan with available AWD. The 3.0L twin-turbo V6 is the sweet spot, and the 2020 CT5-V pairs that engine (360 hp) with a sport tune. Main checks: a 2.0T (LSY) oil-level check and magnetic ride suspension health. The supercharged 6.2L V8 CT5-V Blackwing (668 hp) is a 2022-and-later car — not available for 2020.

What the VIN tells you

8th VIN digit = engine code: 'K' = 2.0L LSY turbo I4 (237 hp, the base engine); 'W' = 3.0L LGY twin-turbo V6 (335 hp in Luxury/Premium Luxury/Sport, 360 hp in the CT5-V). Note the base four is GM's newer LSY, not the older LTG found in the Blazer. The supercharged 6.2L V8 CT5-V Blackwing (668 hp) is a MY2022-and-later car — a genuine 2020 will never decode to it, so treat any 2020 'Blackwing' listing as mislabeled. Confirm the engine and drivetrain (RWD vs. AWD) with a full VIN decode at autovetting.com/decode before applying this checklist.
