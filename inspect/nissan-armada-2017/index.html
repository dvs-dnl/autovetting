The 2nd-gen Armada is based on the Nissan Patrol Y62 — a substantial, body-on-frame full-size SUV. The 5.6L VK56 V8 is extremely reliable and proven across Titan trucks and QX80. Main buyer checks are 4WD transfer-case service, transmission fluid and towing history — not the Takata airbag recall, which has never covered an Armada in any model year. No AFM/MDS cylinder deactivation — just a straightforward V8. Great value versus Sequoia or Expedition at comparable mileage.

What the VIN tells you

There is no engine ambiguity to decode on this car: every U.S. 2017 Armada uses the 5.6L VK56VD direct-injection V8 with the 7-speed automatic — no V6, no diesel, no hybrid and no cylinder deactivation — so the 8th VIN digit will not sort anything useful for you here. What the VIN does matter for is the generation break. The 2017 Armada is an all-new Y62 truck (the global Nissan Patrol), not a facelift of the TA60 Armada that came before it, so a 2017 shares little beyond the badge with a 2015: different frame, different suspension, different interior electronics and different service procedures. Confirm the 10th VIN digit reads ‘H’ for 2017 rather than trusting a listing title, and treat older Armada forum advice and TSBs as non-transferable.
