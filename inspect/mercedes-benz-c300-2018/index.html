2018 Mercedes-Benz C-Class
C300 / C300 4MATIC / C43 AMG (W205, 2015-2021)
High Attention

The W205 C-Class is one of the most satisfying-to-drive executive sedans available used — genuine premium quality, excellent ride, and the 2.0L M274 is a strong engine. But Mercedes ownership costs are above-average: 7G-Tronic transmission service is often skipped, sunroof drain clogs are common, and the electronics suite can generate expensive fault codes. Specialist PPI is required.

Powertrain

M274 timing chain (early B9 builds)

Monitor
Early M274 builds had timing chain adjuster issues. 2016+ with updated tensioner is less prone.
Cold-start rattle check — any sustained rattle = tensioner concern.
Est. cost: $1,200–$2,800 timing chain service Source: M274 service pattern

7G-Tronic automatic fluid service

High Attention
Mercedes marks 7G-Tronic fluid as lifetime — but shudder at 60k+ miles is common without a fluid change. Service at 60k–80k miles is strongly recommended.
Test transmission in stop-and-go and on highway for shudder or hesitation.
Est. cost: $400–$700 fluid service / $3,500–$6,000 transmission Source: Independent Mercedes service consensus

Electrical

COMAND infotainment faults

Monitor
W205 COMAND system has known freeze and navigation errors. Module faults can be expensive.
Test all functions. Scan with Mercedes XENTRY or iCarsoft at a specialist.
Est. cost: $150–$200 PPI scan / $300–$3,000+ faults Source: Mercedes pre-purchase standard

Body

Panoramic sunroof drain clog

High Attention
W205 panoramic sunroof drains are notorious for clogging, causing water to drip into cabin or accumulate in door sills.
Check headliner at A and B-pillar for water stains or musty smell.
Est. cost: $150–$400 drain cleaning / $1,500–$3,000 headliner or electronics damage Source: W205 well-known issue

Suspension

Front control arm and bushing wear

High Attention
W205 control arms wear at 60k–80k miles — similar timeline to BMW.
Front suspension noise check + PPI inspection.
Est. cost: $800–$2,000 front suspension Source: W205 common repair

Documentation

Mercedes specialist PPI — mandatory

Critical
Any used Mercedes requires PPI with XENTRY diagnostic scan.
Budget $150–$250 PPI. Non-negotiable.
Est. cost: $150–$250 PPI / unknown without it Source: Mercedes ownership standard
