What the VIN tells you

The 2020 Venue has one engine: the 1.6L Smartstream Gamma MPI I4 (121 hp), 8th VIN digit '3'. It is front-wheel drive only — there is no turbo and no AWD option — and the 1.6L is paired with Hyundai's IVT, a chain-driven continuously variable transmission. Because there is a single engine family, the 8th digit is a confirmation rather than a fork; the more useful step is running the full VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to verify any open campaign is closed before purchase.
