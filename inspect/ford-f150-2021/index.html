What the VIN tells you

On the 14th-gen F-150 (P702) the engine IS the inspection story, and the 8th VIN digit names it: G = 3.3L Ti-VCT V6 (290 hp, fleet/base — simplest, lowest-risk powertrain); P = 2.7L EcoBoost V6 (325 hp); F = 3.5L EcoBoost V6 (400 hp — the cam-phaser engine to scrutinize hardest; a warm-idle rattle is $1,200–$3,000 of phasers); T = 5.0L Coyote V8 (400 hp — listen for the same phaser tick on cold start); D = 3.5L PowerBoost full hybrid (430 hp — adds a 1.5 kWh battery and the Pro Power Onboard generator to verify). Don't trust the fender badge alone — a 2.7 and a 3.5 EcoBoost look identical from outside. Decode the full VIN at autovetting.com/decode (NHTSA vPIC returns the engine family) before you negotiate. The 10th VIN digit gives the model year: M=2021, N=2022, P=2023, R=2024.
