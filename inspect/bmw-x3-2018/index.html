sDrive30i / xDrive30i / M40i (G01, 2018-2024)
High Attention

The G01 X3 (2018+) is BMW's best X3 yet — the B48 engine is significantly more reliable than predecessor N20 units and the B58 6-cylinder is excellent. Main buyer concerns: electric water pump/thermostat failure (all BMW B-series engines), control arm bushing wear front and rear, and maintenance costs. BMW specialist PPI with iDrive scan is required on any used G01 purchase.

Powertrain

Electric water pump and thermostat failure

High Attention
BMW electric water pumps fail at 80k–120k miles on B48 and B58 engines. Failure without warning can cause rapid overheating.
Check coolant level cold and inspect expansion tank for cracks. Any coolant loss = suspect water pump.
Est. cost: $600–$1,200 water pump + thermostat Source: BMW electric water pump known pattern

Oil consumption (B48/B58)

Monitor
BMW considers up to 1 qt per 1,000 miles as within spec. Check dipstick before every purchase.
Pull dipstick. Ask seller how often they add oil between changes.
Est. cost: Monitor Source: BMW consumption standard

Chassis

Front and rear control arm bushing wear

High Attention
G01 control arm bushings wear at 60k–80k miles. Steering wander and noise over bumps are symptoms.
Test straight-line tracking at highway speed. Consult PPI for bushing condition.
Est. cost: $800–$2,500 front and rear suspension refresh Source: G01 common repair list

Electronics

iDrive faults and module errors

Monitor
G01 iDrive accumulates fault codes. Scan with BMW ISTA before purchase.
PPI with ISTA scan is non-negotiable.
Est. cost: $150–$200 PPI / $300–$2,000+ module faults Source: BMW pre-purchase standard

AWD

xDrive transfer case fluid

Monitor
Transfer case fluid service every 50k miles — often skipped at dealer.
Ask for transfer case service history.
Est. cost: $200–$400 fluid service Source: BMW AWD service standard

Documentation

BMW specialist PPI required

Critical
Non-negotiable on any used BMW. ISTA scan reveals all fault codes and deferred maintenance.
$150–$200 PPI at BMW-trained independent shop before any commitment.
Est. cost: $150–$200 PPI / unknown without it Source: BMW ownership standard
