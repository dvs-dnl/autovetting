S / SV / SL (J11, US 2017-2022)
Watchful

The Rogue Sport (Qashqai) is the subcompact sibling to the Rogue — smaller, lower base price, same CVT platform. The 2.0L MR20 is less powerful than the Rogue's 2.5L but more fuel-efficient. CVT fluid service (NS-3 every 30k miles) is the identical critical maintenance item. Strong value for buyers who don't need Rogue cargo space.
