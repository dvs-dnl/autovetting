2018 Nissan Maxima
SV / SL / Platinum / SR (A36, 8th gen, 2016-2023)
Watchful

The A36 Maxima markets itself as a 4-door sports car — the 3.5L VQ35DE is genuinely powerful. The CVT is the primary risk: under a powerful V6, the belt wears faster than in lighter vehicles. CVT fluid service with genuine NS-3 fluid every 30k miles is essential. Outstanding value for buyers who want near-luxury sport sedan performance at a mainstream price.

Powertrain

Xtronic CVT under V6 load

High Attention
The CVT paired with the 300hp VQ35 is under more load than in lighter Nissan CVT cars. NS-3 fluid every 30k miles is non-negotiable.
Request CVT fluid service receipts. Test hard acceleration for any belt slip or shudder.
Est. cost: $200–$350 service / $3,500–$5,500 CVT replacement Source: Nissan CVT service standard

VQ35DE oil consumption

Monitor
VQ35 can consume oil above 100k miles.
Pull dipstick before test drive.
Est. cost: Monitor Source: VQ35 pattern

Electrical

NissanConnect infotainment / navigation

Monitor
NissanConnect has known freeze and slow-response issues.
Test all infotainment functions.
Est. cost: $0 update / $1,000–$2,000 head unit Source: Nissan campaigns

Suspension

Sport suspension wear (SR)

Monitor
Sport suspension in SR trim is firmer — struts wear faster on rough roads.
Test ride quality and bounce each corner.
Est. cost: $400–$800 per strut Source: Common wear

Body

Paint quality and finish

Low Risk
A36 paint quality is above average.
Standard body inspection.
Est. cost: $0–$300 touch-up Source: Standard inspection

Documentation

CVT fluid receipts + recall check

Critical
CVT history under V6 is the defining purchase variable.
Run nhtsa.gov/recalls. Require complete CVT service history.
Est. cost: Walk away from CVT without documented service Source: NHTSA
