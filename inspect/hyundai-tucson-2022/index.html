What the VIN tells you

The 4th-gen Tucson (NX4) offers three powertrains, and the 8th VIN digit is the fastest way to separate the gas car from the hybrids. 8th digit 'E' = the 2.5L Smartstream G2.5 MPI four-cylinder (187 hp) paired with a conventional 8-speed automatic — NOT a CVT, and critically NOT a Theta II engine, so the rod-bearing / engine-fire recall anxiety that haunts older 2.0T and 2.4 Hyundais does not apply here. The 1.6L turbo hybrids carry a different code (reported as '1' for the full HEV — treat as confirm-at-vPIC, single-source) and use a 6-speed automatic, not an eCVT: the HEV makes 226 hp combined and the plug-in PHEV (261 hp combined, 13.8 kWh battery) is a separate build identified by its charge port. On any hybrid, verify high-voltage battery state-of-health. Whichever powertrain, run the full 17-digit VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm open NX4 campaigns are closed; 10th digit N = 2022. Decode the full VIN at autovetting.com/decode (NHTSA vPIC) to confirm engine family and drivetrain.
