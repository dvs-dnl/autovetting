2018 Buick Enclave
Preferred / Essence / Premium / Avenir (C1, 2nd gen, 2018-2024)
Watchful

2nd-gen Enclave (2018+) is a premium 3-row crossover sharing the C1 platform with the Chevrolet Traverse. The 3.6L LGX V6 is a strong engine but the 9-speed auto had early hesitation and shudder issues addressed by TSBs. Direct injection carbon buildup on intake valves is a known long-term service item. Premium interior quality is genuine — one of the better luxury-adjacent values in its class.

Powertrain

9-speed Hydra-Matic hesitation / shudder

High Attention
The 9T65 9-speed auto had documented hesitation and shudder on 2018 first-year builds. TSB updates improved behavior significantly.
Test transmission in all driving conditions — stop-and-go, highway on-ramp, light-throttle cruise.
Est. cost: $350–$550 fluid flush + calibration / $3,500–$5,500 transmission Source: GM 9T65 TSB series

3.6L LGX GDI carbon buildup

Monitor
Direct injection causes carbon deposits on intake valves past 80k miles. Loss of power and rough idle are symptoms.
Test idle quality and acceleration smoothness.
Est. cost: $400–$800 walnut blast intake cleaning Source: GDI carbon pattern

AWD

Rear coupling service (AWD models)

Monitor
AWD coupling fluid service every 45k miles. Neglect causes vibration.
Test highway cornering for vibration.
Est. cost: $150–$300 service / $800–$1,500 coupling Source: AWD service requirement

Electrical

Infotainment and wireless features

Low Risk
2nd-gen Enclave infotainment is generally reliable.
Test CarPlay, navigation, heated seats, and all zone HVAC controls.
Est. cost: $0 update Source: Buick campaigns
