High Attention

2nd-gen Q5 (2018+) on the MLB Evo platform is well-engineered and premium — but like all Audi, maintenance costs are above-average. EA888 timing chain tensioner and coolant flange are the known failure modes. DSG fluid service is often skipped. SQ5 with 3.0T V6 adds complexity. Audi specialist PPI with VCDS scan is required.

Powertrain

EA888 timing chain tensioner

High Attention
Same timing chain tensioner concern as Audi A4 — failure at 60k–100k miles on neglected oil.
Cold-start rattle check (first 5 seconds).
Est. cost: $1,200–$2,500 timing chain service Source: EA888 gen3 pattern

Coolant flange (plastic) failure

High Attention
Plastic coolant flange cracks and causes rapid coolant loss — a well-known FY Q5 failure.
Check coolant level and inspect engine bay for coolant smell.
Est. cost: $300–$600 coolant flange + flush Source: Audi FY Q5 documented issue

Transmission

DSG fluid service

High Attention
S tronic DSG requires fluid service every 40k miles. Shudder at low speed = overdue.
Test stop-and-go under 15 mph for shudder or jerk.
Est. cost: $400–$700 DSG service / $2,000–$4,000 mechatronic Source: Audi DSG standard

AWD

Quattro / Haldex coupling service

Monitor
Rear differential and coupling fluid service required every 30k miles.
Test highway cornering for vibration.
Est. cost: $200–$400 service Source: Audi Quattro standard

Electronics

MMI faults and Virtual Cockpit

Monitor
VCDS scan before purchase reveals accumulated fault codes.
PPI with VCDS at Audi specialist.
Est. cost: $150–$200 PPI scan Source: Audi pre-purchase standard
