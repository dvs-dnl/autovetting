AFM Lifter Failure (V8 models)

5.3L / 6.2L AFM lifter failure

High Risk on high-mileage V8
Active Fuel Management (cylinder deactivation) causes accelerated lifter wear on sustained highway driving at 55–75 mph. Symptoms: AFM tick or knock after warm-up. Very common failure at 80k–120k miles.
Warm engine fully, then listen at idle for a ticking/knocking sound. Any persistent tick = AFM lifter damage possible. Ask if AFM has been disabled.
Est. cost: $2,500–$5,000 lifter replacement + DOD delete Source: GM AFM/DOD lifter failure pattern

AFM disable tune or Range AFM device

Low Risk
Many informed owners disable AFM with a Range AFM Disabler ($60) or tuner. A car with AFM disabled and documented = significantly lower lifter risk.
Ask if seller has disabled AFM. OBD port dongle or tune = good sign for lifter longevity.
Est. cost: $60 Range AFM Disabler (preventive) Source: GM V8 owner best-practice

NHTSA Recalls (verify completion via VIN)

25V-274
6.2L V8 Connecting Rod / Crankshaft - engine failure
2021-2024 Silverado 1500 built with the 6.2L V8 only: connecting-rod and crankshaft manufacturing defects can damage and destroy the engine. Dealer inspects and repairs or replaces it; trucks that pass get higher-viscosity oil, a new oil filler cap and a new filter. This is the most expensive item on this page by an order of magnitude - on a 6.2L truck, confirm by VIN that it was inspected or the engine replaced before any money moves. 5.3L and 3.0L trucks are not in scope.
24V-797
Diesel Transmission Control Valve - rear wheels lock
2020-2022 Silverado 1500 with a diesel engine only: the transmission control valve can fail and lock the rear wheels. Dealer installs new transmission control module software, and GM covers the transmission repair where that software finds a defective valve. Gas trucks are outside the scope.
26V-083
Re-repair of 24V-797 - done wrong the first time
2020-2022 Silverado 1500 that may have been repaired incorrectly under 24V-797, leaving the control valve still able to fail and lock the rear wheels. So a seller saying the transmission recall was done is answering for 24V-797 and not for this one - both have to read closed. VINs became searchable 2026-02-12 and owner letters went out 2026-03-06, so a truck sold before that will not have it.
22V-903
Daytime Running Lights Stay Lit With Headlights
2022-2023 Silverado 1500 among eleven GM lines: body-control-module software can leave the daytime running lights on when the headlights are on, glaring oncoming traffic - an FMVSS 108 failure. Dealer or over-the-air software update.
22V-463
Accessory Sport Bar Blocks the High-Mount Brake Light
2022 Silverado 1500 and Sierra fitted with the accessory sport bar: the bar high-mounted brake light may not work and the bar can block the factory high-mount lamp - an FMVSS 108 failure. Only applies if the truck actually carries that bar, so look at the bed before chasing it. Dealer supplies the correct wiring.
22V-873
Airbag Module Left in Manufacturing Mode
Certain 2022 Silverado 1500 whose sensing diagnostic module was never activated at the assembly plant, so the airbags would not deploy in a crash - an FMVSS 208 failure. Closed before first sale: the trucks went back to the GM plant and no owner letters were mailed. Listed here so a used buyer recognises it as already settled rather than an open item.
