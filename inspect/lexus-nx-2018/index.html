2018 Lexus NX
200t / 300 / 300h Hybrid / F Sport (AZ10, 1st gen, 2015-2021)
Watchful

The 1st-gen Lexus NX (2015–2021) brought Lexus into the compact luxury SUV segment. The 2.0T 8AR-FTS is less proven than Toyota's naturally-aspirated engines and has documented oil consumption reports. The 300h hybrid system is the more reliable choice and avoids the turbo concerns. Lexus quality is still above average even on the turbo — just verify oil condition and check service records.

Powertrain

8AR-FTS 2.0T oil consumption

High Attention
The 8AR-FTS turbocharged engine has documented oil consumption reports — some engines consume 1 qt per 1,000–2,000 miles above 80k.
Pull dipstick before test drive. Below MIN on a recent change = active consumption.
Est. cost: $0 if early / $6,000–$9,000 engine rebuild Source: Lexus 8AR-FTS consumption reports

NX 300h hybrid battery health

Low Risk
Hybrid system is Toyota-proven and durable. Should achieve 33+ mpg combined.
Test fuel economy.
Est. cost: $2,000–$3,500 battery if needed Source: Toyota hybrid reliability
