LX / EX / SX / SXL (4th gen, 2016–2020)
Monitor

The 4th-gen Optima is a well-styled mid-sizer let down by the Theta II engine recall. Both the 2.4L and 2.0T are subject to NHTSA Theta II engine recalls (engine seizure due to debris from manufacturing). Always confirm the recall is closed before purchase. Post-recall cars with clean oil history are reliable; skipped oil changes still cause problems despite the recall fix.

Engine Recall — Check First

Theta II engine seizure recall — verify by VIN

Critical — Verify Closed
Kia/Hyundai Theta II engines (2.4L and 2.0T) were subject to a catastrophic engine seizure recall due to metal debris from manufacturing contaminating the crankshaft bearings. Engines can seize without warning. Multiple Theta II engine campaigns cover the 2016 Optima — confirm coverage by VIN.
Run VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. If the recall shows open, do NOT purchase until dealer completes the repair. Ask for the recall completion paperwork.
Est. cost: Free dealer repair (engine replacement if seized) Source: NHTSA / Kia Theta II engine recall (verify by VIN)

Knock sensor monitoring system (KSDS)

Monitor
Kia installed a Knock Sensor Detection System on post-recall cars to monitor for bearing noise. If the KSDS warning light illuminates, the engine needs immediate inspection.
Check for any MIL or KSDS warning on the instrument cluster.
Est. cost: $0 if caught early; engine replacement if ignored Source: NHTSA / Kia KSDS software

Transmission

6-speed automatic (A6MF2)

Low Risk
The 6AT on the Optima is generally reliable. Test for smooth shifts and no hunting between gears.
Test all gear ranges from cold start. No hesitation or flare should be present.
Est. cost: $150–$200 fluid service Source: Standard drivetrain check

7-speed DCT (2.0T models)

Monitor
The dual-clutch transmission on SX Turbo models can shudder at low speed. Software updates address many cases.
Test slow creep in traffic. Any shudder or jerkiness at low speed = check TSBs for update.
Est. cost: $0 software update; $2,000+ clutch pack if worn Source: Kia DCT TSBs

Electrical & Comfort

UVO infotainment / Android Auto

Low Risk
UVO system is generally solid on 4th-gen. Test CarPlay/Android Auto and all buttons.
Test all infotainment and heated seat functions.
Est. cost: $0 Source: Standard check

Sunroof drain clogs

Low Risk
Panoramic sunroof drains can clog, leading to water intrusion at the A-pillar.
Check headliner for water stains near A-pillar if sunroof-equipped.
Est. cost: $50–$150 flush Source: Common sunroof pattern

Documentation

Oil change records + recall paperwork

High Risk
Post-recall Optimas still suffer engine issues if oil changes were skipped. Both the recall completion paperwork and oil records are essential on this car.
Request all service records. No records on a Theta II car = significant risk.
Est. cost: n/a Source: AutoVetting editorial
