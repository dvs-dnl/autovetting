Timing Belt — Critical Maintenance

What the VIN tells you

On the 2019 S60 the 8th VIN digit is the trim character, not the engine, and this one is measured both ways, which makes it unusually safe to lean on. The engine rides in positions 4 through 7. Single-variable decode at vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov: '7JRA22T' returns engine 'B4204TS (HP)', 316 hp, the turbocharged-and-supercharged T6; '7JR102F' returns 'B4204T (MP)', 250 hp with an 8-speed automatic, which is the T5. Hold either one and vary position 8 and the same three-way map comes back on both: 'K' = Momentum, 'L' = Inscription, 'M' = R-Design. So position 8 never moves the engine, and a given character means the same trim whichever engine you are looking at. Real-world anchor: 7JR102FL1KG003342 is advertised by its selling dealer as a 2019 S60 T5 Inscription, exactly what '102F' plus 'L' predicts. Two reading cautions, because NHTSA's Volvo submission is noisy here. The Series field on T5 patterns comes back as 'FWD/eAWD (T8)', which reads like it is telling you the car is a T8 and is not - go by EngineModel and EngineHP instead. And DriveType is unreliable on this car: it returned blank on one T5 pattern and 'AWD' on two others, when the 2019 T5 is front-drive and only the T6 and T8 are all-wheel drive. The fork changes the inspection: the T6 carries a supercharger ahead of its turbo, a system the T5 does not have at all, and the T8 adds a high-voltage pack and charging system that none of the gas items below cover.
