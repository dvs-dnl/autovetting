Powertrain

2.0T VTEC oil dilution in cold climates

Monitor
The K20C4 has a mild oil dilution profile in very cold climates — less severe than the 1.5T L15B7 but present. Check if car came from a cold-weather state.
Check oil level and smell on 2.0T. Gasoline odor in oil of a cold-climate car = dilution.
Est. cost: $0 if mild / $5,500+ if severe Source: Acura 2.0T cold-climate pattern

AWD

SH-AWD rear differential fluid (critical)

High Attention
SH-AWD rear diff requires Honda DPS-F fluid every 30k miles. Neglect causes shudder on tight turns.
Test tight parking lot turns at full lock — any vibration = fluid needed.
Est. cost: $150–$300 fluid service / $1,500–$3,000 rear diff Source: Acura SH-AWD standard

What the VIN tells you

The 3rd-gen RDX (2019–2025, chassis TC1/TC2) shipped with a single engine — the 2.0L turbo K20C4 (272 hp) with the 10-speed automatic — so the engine-encoding 8th VIN digit is uniform across every 2019 RDX and won't separate trims. The decode that actually matters on this generation is drivetrain: front-wheel drive versus torque-vectoring SH-AWD, which changes the model portion of the VIN rather than the 8th digit — confirm it against the badge and build sheet, because SH-AWD adds the rear-differential fluid service you want documented. All 2019 trims (Base / Technology / A-Spec / Advance) share the same powertrain; only equipment differs. Priorities on every 2019 VIN regardless of decode: the 2.0T's mild cold-climate oil-dilution profile, the SH-AWD rear-diff fluid history (AWD cars), and the 23V-858 Denso fuel-pump recall — verify that campaign is closed by VIN.
