2018 Chevrolet Suburban
LS / LT / Premier (K2XX, 2015-2020; 3/4-ton equivalent)
High Attention

The Suburban is the Tahoe with a 21-inch longer wheelbase — same platform, same AFM lifter risk - but not the same engines: the 6.2L was never offered in the Suburban, so every 2018 example is the 5.3L L83, and it has AFM. The extended wheelbase provides genuine 3-row space and 8,300 lb tow rating. Budget the AFM lifter repair ($3,500–$7,000) as a near-certain cost on any high-mileage example. Preferred play: find one where AFM has already been deleted.

What the VIN tells you

On the K2XX Suburban the 8th VIN digit is the engine position, but it does not fork anything on this model - and the character that forks the Tahoe is not legal here at all. Single-variable decodes at vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov for model year 2018, on two independent base patterns, 1GNSKJK (4WD) and 1GNSCJK (4x2): C returns engine L83 - SIDI: Spark Ignited Direct Injection, VVT: Variable Valve Timing, AFM, E85 MAX, ALUM at 5.3L on both. J, the character that returns the 6.2L L86 on a Tahoe and on a Yukon pattern, comes back on a Suburban pattern with error 14 and every engine field blank. Published coverage says the same thing in plain language: GM Authority reported in April 2017 that the 2018 Suburban goes without the 6.2L L86 V-8, the 10L80 10-speed automatic and the specially-tuned Magnetic Ride Control suspension, because the Suburban got the RST appearance package without the Tahoe's Performance Package. So the engine field on this entry was corrected: there is one engine in a 2018 Suburban 1500, the 5.3L L83, and the 6L80 fluid-shudder item below therefore applies to every example rather than to a subset - the opposite of the Tahoe, where that same item is a 5.3L-only test. The trap on this car runs the other way. G at position 8 does resolve, to L96 - E85 MAX, Iron at 6.0L, and that is the fleet Suburban 3500HD engine: a separate heavy-duty series rated 360 hp in the published 2018 3500HD specification, not a 1500 option. Decode 1GNSKJKG and vPIC will happily build you a 1500 Premier with an engine never sold in one. B at position 8 returns error 14 and blanks the engine fields the same way J does. Read the whole 17-character VIN, treat the series badge as part of the identification, and do not read the AdditionalErrorText line saying Unused position(s): 8 as evidence of anything - that sentence is generic wrapper text and has already been measured flatly wrong on Nissan and GM responses whose named position decided the engine. One cross-reference, because this catalog carries both generations of this nameplate: the 2021 Suburban entry maps position 8 as D for the 5.3L L84, L for the 6.2L L87 and T for the 3.0L LM2 Duramax, all on the T1XX platform. Not one of those characters is this car. K2XX (2015-2020) and T1XX (2021 on) share a badge and nothing about their position-8 map, so never carry a reading across the 2020/2021 line.
