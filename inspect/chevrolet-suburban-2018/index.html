2018 Chevrolet Suburban
LS / LT / Premier (K2XX, 2015-2020; 3/4-ton equivalent)
High Attention

The Suburban is the Tahoe with a 21-inch longer wheelbase — same platform, same engines, same AFM lifter risk. All Suburban V8s have AFM. The extended wheelbase provides genuine 3-row space and 8,300 lb tow rating. Budget the AFM lifter repair ($3,500–$7,000) as a near-certain cost on any high-mileage example. Preferred play: find one where AFM has already been deleted.

Powertrain

AFM lifter failure — mandatory check

Critical
Same AFM lifter failure as Tahoe and Silverado. All Suburban V8s use AFM. Lifters fail at 60k–100k on neglected oil.
Cold-start + listen for ticking. Scan for P0300 codes. Request oil interval documentation.
Est. cost: $3,500–$7,000 lifter replacement Source: K2XX AFM pattern

6L80 transmission

Monitor
Shudder at highway light throttle = fluid issue.
Test light throttle at 35–50 mph.
Est. cost: $350–$550 fluid flush Source: 6L80 service pattern

Frame & Body

Frame rust and undercarriage inspection

High Attention
Large BOF vehicles from salt states accumulate rust on frame rails and cross-members.
Full undercarriage inspection — focus on rear frame and spare tire carrier.
Est. cost: Walk away from structural rust Source: Truck inspection standard

Suspension

Electronic level control (ELC) air system

Monitor
Rear air leveling system airbags and compressor wear at 100k+ miles.
Verify rear sits level side-to-side and height adjusts.
Est. cost: $400–$900 per airbag / $600–$1,200 compressor Source: Common Suburban wear

Third Row

Power fold third-row operation

Low Risk
Power-fold third row can fail to fold/unfold reliably.
Cycle third row through all positions.
Est. cost: $200–$500 mechanism Source: Common wear

Documentation

AFM documentation + recall check

Critical
AFM history is the defining variable in Suburban value.
Run VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. Demand AFM history documentation.
Est. cost: Walk away from AFM-undocumented high-mileage Suburban Source: NHTSA
