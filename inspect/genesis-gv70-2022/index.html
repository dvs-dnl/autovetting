What the VIN tells you

The 2022 GV70 (JK1) is sold with two gas engines that carry different inspection priorities, and trim is the fastest way to tell them apart: Standard, Advanced, and Advanced Plus use the 2.5L Smartstream G2.5T turbo four (300 hp), while Sport and Sport Plus use the 3.5L Lambda III twin-turbo V6 (375 hp); both drive an 8-speed automatic. On the 3.5T, prioritize a cold-start check for any turbo or oil-feed concerns and confirm the 8-speed shifts cleanly; the 2.5T is the simpler, lower-cost-to-own choice. In Genesis/Hyundai VINs the 8th digit encodes the engine — 'B' is commonly reported for the 2.5T and 'C' for the 3.5T — but confirm the engine for your specific VIN at autovetting.com/decode or vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov rather than relying on the letter alone. (The Electrified GV70 EV arrived as a later model year, so a 2022 car is gas-only.) Run the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls before purchase.
