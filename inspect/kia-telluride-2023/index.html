What the VIN tells you

The first-gen Telluride (ON, 2020–2025) has just one engine — the 3.8L Lambda II GDI V6 (291 hp) paired with an 8-speed automatic; there is no four-cylinder, no turbo and no CVT in any Telluride, FWD or AWD. The 8th VIN digit confirms it: C = the 3.8L Lambda II V6. This V6 is unrelated to the Theta II 2.0T/2.4L engines behind Kia's KSDS bearing-wear recalls, so the Telluride carries none of that exposure. Position 10 encodes model year on Kia's scheme: P = 2023 (N = 2022, R = 2024). Confirm the decode at vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov before applying this checklist.
