The AX10 C-HR is Toyota's most distinctive styling departure — available only in FWD with a 2.0L engine and CVT. Extremely reliable with no significant failure modes documented. Limited power (144hp) is the honest tradeoff. No AWD is a real limitation for snowy climates. Outstanding reliability for urban buyers who prioritize style and low ownership cost.

What the VIN tells you

The 2018 US C-HR is single-powertrain across every trim (LE/XLE/XLE Premium): one 2.0L 3ZR-FAE Valvematic four (144 hp US-spec) with a CVT, FWD only — no AWD and no alternate engine on the US car. The 8th VIN digit therefore does not fork an engine here; decoding only confirms a genuine North-American 3ZR-FAE C-HR. Any listing claiming AWD or a turbo is a non-US import or mislabeled.
