What the VIN tells you

The 2021 Seltos (1st-gen SP2) comes with two very different powertrains, and the 8th VIN digit separates them: 'A' = the 2.0L MPI Nu four-cylinder (146 hp), naturally aspirated and paired with Kia's IVT (a chain-driven CVT); '2' = the 1.6L turbo Gamma four-cylinder (175 hp) paired with a 7-speed dual-clutch (DCT). The inspection paths diverge: the 2.0L/IVT is the simpler, lower-risk choice (verify IVT fluid service, no turbo concerns), while the 1.6T/DCT can shudder at 15-25 mph — request the dealer DCT software-update history and feel for clutch shudder in slow traffic (a worn DCT clutch pack is a $2,000+ repair). The turbo SX trim adds AWD as standard; AWD is optional on lower trims. Neither engine is a Theta II unit, so the Hyundai-Kia engine-fire campaigns do not apply here. Decode the full VIN at vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov to confirm engine family, and run it at nhtsa.gov for open recalls before purchase.
