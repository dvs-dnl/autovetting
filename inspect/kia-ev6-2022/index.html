2022 Kia EV6
Light / Standard / Standard Plus / Wind / GT-Line / GT (1st gen, 2022–present)
Low Risk

The EV6 is one of the best EVs in the segment — 800V architecture enables ultra-fast 350 kW charging (10–80% in under 18 minutes), exceptional driving dynamics, and a spacious interior on the E-GMP platform shared with the Hyundai Ioniq 5. No widespread battery or drivetrain failures on used examples. Main buyer checks: software version (OTA updates are frequent), confirm 400V vs 800V charging compatibility, and battery state of health. On recalls, NHTSA lists four campaigns for MY2022. Two matter: the ICCU 12-volt charging failure that can cause a loss of drive power, which ran twice (24V-200, then 24V-867 expanding and replacing it, so a car fixed under the first still needs the second), and 26V-431, a high-voltage-battery fire campaign with a park-outside instruction whose population is only 8 cars — negligible odds, but a VIN check is the only way to rule it out.

Battery & Charging

HV battery state of health

Monitor
Request a battery health check at a Kia dealer — the 77.4 kWh Long Range battery should show >90% SoH on low-mileage examples. Degradation is typically slow on E-GMP.
Check displayed range at 100% charge vs rated range. Any >15% deficit on a sub-50k example = investigate.

NHTSA Recalls (verify completion via VIN)

26V-431
Severity: Critical — fire, park outside
High-voltage battery cells may have misaligned electrodes, which can short and cause a fire while parked or driving. The population is 8 vehicles across the 2022-2024 EV6 and 2024 EV9 — narrow enough that only a VIN check can answer it — Kia SC375, report received 2026-07-02. Until repaired Kia says park outside away from structures and limit charging to 80 percent. Remedy: high-voltage battery system assembly replaced free.
24V-867
Severity: Critical — loss of drive power
The Integrated Charging Control Unit can be damaged and stop charging the 12-volt battery, causing a loss of drive power while driving. 62,872 units, Kia SC327, report received 2024-11-18. This expands and replaces 24V-200, so a car already repaired under that campaign still needs this remedy. Remedy: ICCU and its fuse inspected and replaced as needed plus a software update, free.
24V-200
Severity: Superseded by 24V-867
The first form of the same ICCU 12-volt charging failure — 48,232 units, Kia SC302, report received 2024-03-14. Confirm the car carries the later SC327 remedy and not only this one. Remedy: ICCU and fuse inspected and replaced as needed plus a software update, free.
22V-322
Severity: High — rollaway
A Shifter Control Unit software error can disengage the parking mechanism and let the vehicle roll away. 9,014 units, Kia SC236, report received 2022-05-11. Remedy: Shifter Control Unit software updated at no cost.

What the VIN tells you

The 2022 EV6 is electric, so the 8th VIN digit encodes battery and motor rather than a combustion engine — and the difference is worth real money. 8th digit ‘B’ = 58 kWh battery, single rear motor, 167 hp (the Standard-Range RWD ‘Light’ trim) — shortest range (~232 mi EPA) and the one to skip if you road-trip. 8th digit ‘A’ = 77.4 kWh battery, single rear motor, 225 hp (Long-Range RWD Wind/GT-Line) — the sweet spot at ~310 mi. 8th digit ‘C’ = 77.4 kWh battery, dual-motor AWD, 320 hp (~274 mi). All three share the same 800V E-GMP architecture and 350 kW fast-charging. Confirm battery state-of-health at a Kia dealer (free) — Long-Range cars should read >90% SoH on low-mileage examples — and verify the ICCU/charging-relay recall (select 2022) is closed.
