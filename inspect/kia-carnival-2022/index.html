NHTSA Recalls (verify completion via VIN)

21V-277
Fuel Rail Tube Damage (Fire Risk)
Plastic fuel rail tube may have been damaged in assembly and can leak fuel — 2022 Carnival, campaign SC211. Dealer inspects and replaces the multi-port injection fuel rail if needed.
21V-908
Sliding Door Latch Failure
Passenger-side power sliding door remote controller can fail to latch the door, letting it open while moving — 2022 Carnival, SC221. Dealer replaces the release actuator drum and holder.
23V-179
Tow Hitch Harness Short (Fire Risk)
Water on the tow hitch harness module circuit board can short and start a fire — 2022-2023 Carnival with the Kia accessory hitch, SC265. Park outside until remedied; dealer installs a fuse and wire-extension kit.
23V-236
Sliding Door Auto-Reverse
Power sliding door auto-reverse may not activate in all situations and can close on an occupant — all 2022 Carnival, SC266. Dealer updates the door control module software.
24V-025
Roof Molding Detachment
Roof molding can loosen and detach into the road — 2022-2024 Carnival, SC292. Dealer inspects and secures or replaces the molding.
26V-232
Fuel Pipe Connection Leak (Fire Risk)
Fuel can leak at the fuel-pipe-to-fuel-rail connection — 2022-2026 Carnival, SC368 (April 2026 campaign). Dealer tightens or replaces the fuel pipe.

What the VIN tells you

Every U.S. 2022 Carnival (KA4) uses the same 3.5L Smartstream/Lambda III V6 GDI (290 hp) with an 8-speed automatic — there is no four-cylinder or hybrid option this model year (the Carnival Hybrid did not arrive until 2025), so the 8th VIN digit carries no engine-family ambiguity to decode. That is reassuring: this V6 is NOT part of Kia’s Theta II / KSDS engine-fire population, so the rod-bearing knock-sensor recall anxiety that haunts 2.0T and 2.4 Kias does not apply here. What the VIN still matters for is recalls — run the full 17-digit VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls before purchase to confirm any open KA4 campaigns (early seat-belt-anchor and power-sliding-door actions) are closed.
