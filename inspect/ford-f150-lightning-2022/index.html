The F-150 Lightning is Ford's electric truck — practical, powerful, and capable of bidirectional charging for home backup power (Intelligent Backup Power). Early production had multiple suspension and electrical recalls. The frunk is genuinely useful. Battery range is lower than EPA in cold weather. Ford has been active with OTA updates and dealer repairs. Verify all recalls are closed — there were significant early-production issues.

Critical — Run Full VIN Check
