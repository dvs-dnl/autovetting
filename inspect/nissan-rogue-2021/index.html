In the U.S. it came with a single engine — the proven 2.5L QR25DE four-cylinder (181 hp) — paired with an Xtronic CVT; the 1.5L VC-Turbo did not arrive until 2022. What the VIN tells you

The 2021 Rogue is the launch year of the third-generation (T33) redesign, and in the U.S. it came with only one engine — the 2.5L QR25DE four-cylinder (181 hp) driving an Xtronic CVT. The 1.5L VC-Turbo three-cylinder that buyers associate with this body style did NOT arrive until the 2022 model year, so any 2021 listing advertising a “1.5 turbo” is misidentified — confirm the engine with a full VIN decode at vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov. Position 10 of the VIN encodes the model year (M = 2021); a car decoding to N (2022) or later is the VC-Turbo car and follows a different inspection path (oil-consumption checks). For a verified 2021, the priorities are the proven QR25DE (no turbo concerns) and the third-gen Xtronic CVT — request CVT fluid-service records and test for shudder-free acceleration. Run the full VIN at nhtsa.gov to confirm recall history before purchase.
