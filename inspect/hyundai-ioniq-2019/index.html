2019 Hyundai Ioniq
Blue / SEL / Limited / Plug-In / Electric (AE, 2017-2022)
Low Risk

The AE Ioniq is the most fuel-efficient non-plugin hybrid available — 58 mpg combined on the Blue trim. The smaller 28 kWh battery on the Ioniq Electric gives only 124 miles — adequate for urban use but less than Bolt or Model 3. Hybrid DCT fluid service is the critical maintenance item. Outstanding total cost of ownership for city commuters.
