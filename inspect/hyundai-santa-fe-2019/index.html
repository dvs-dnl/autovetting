2019 Hyundai Santa Fe
SE / SEL / Limited / Ultimate (TM, 4th gen, 2019-2023)
High Attention

The TM Santa Fe (2019+) refreshed the model but retained the same engine concerns as prior generations. The 2.4L Nu and 2.0T Theta II engines are both subject to KSDS recall campaigns. Any unresolved engine campaign is a walk-away. AWD coupling fluid service is the most commonly missed maintenance item. Strong family SUV value when recall history is fully documented.

Engine KSDS recall (Nu / Theta II) — multiple campaigns

Powertrain

Nu / Theta II engine KSDS recall — critical

Critical
Both Santa Fe engines are in the KSDS recall family for rod bearing wear leading to seizure. Multiple campaigns apply.
Run VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls BEFORE any test drive. All KSDS campaigns must be complete.
Est. cost: $0 under recall / $6,000–$9,000 engine seizure Source: Hyundai KSDS recall family

2.0T Theta II oil maintenance

High Attention
The Theta II requires synthetic oil every 5k miles and is extremely sensitive to neglect.
Check oil condition and request full service records on 2.0T.
Est. cost: $6,000–$9,000 engine if neglected Source: Hyundai 2.0T service requirement

AWD

HTRAC AWD coupling fluid

High Attention
HTRAC AWD coupling requires fluid every 30k miles — almost universally skipped.
Test highway cornering for vibration or shudder. Request fluid service receipt.
Est. cost: $150–$300 fluid service / $800–$1,800 coupling Source: Hyundai AWD maintenance

Electrical

AVN infotainment and BlueLink

Low Risk
AVN has known connectivity issues. BlueLink may require subscription.
Test all infotainment functions.
Est. cost: $0 update Source: Hyundai campaigns

Suspension

Rear MDPS software update

Monitor
Some TM Santa Fes received a motor-driven power steering software update for steering feel.
Test steering feel at all speeds — confirm update is on record.
Est. cost: $0 update Source: Hyundai TSB

Documentation

ALL engine recall campaigns + AWD service — both required

Critical
Two non-negotiable documents: engine KSDS recall completion proof + AWD fluid service history.
Run nhtsa.gov/recalls. Walk away from any open engine campaign.
Est. cost: Walk away from unresolved KSDS campaigns Source: NHTSA
