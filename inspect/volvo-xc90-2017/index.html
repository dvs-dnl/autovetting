2017 Volvo XC90
Momentum / Inscription / R-Design / T8 PHEV (SPA, 2nd gen, 2016-present)
High Attention

The 2nd-gen XC90 on the SPA platform is one of the most beautiful and technologically advanced SUVs at this price point. But Volvo SPA ownership carries real maintenance costs. The Drive-E 4-cylinder (T5/T6) has timing belt/chain service requirements; the T8 PHEV adds HV battery complexity. Sensus infotainment has known reliability issues. Budget $3,000–$5,000/year for maintenance and require a Volvo specialist PPI.

Powertrain

Drive-E timing belt/chain service (T5/T6)

High Attention
T5/T6 Drive-E engines use a timing belt that requires replacement at 100k miles or 10 years.
Request timing belt service record on any T5/T6 with 80k+ miles.
Est. cost: $800–$1,500 timing belt service Source: Volvo Drive-E service requirement

T8 PHEV battery health (if equipped)

Monitor
T8 18.8 kWh battery degrades — should provide at least 17 miles EV range (22 EPA).
Charge to full and test EV-only range.
Est. cost: $6,000–$12,000 HV battery Source: T8 battery wear pattern

Electrical

Sensus infotainment freeze and reboot

High Attention
Sensus is the most commonly complained-about feature on the SPA XC90. Freeze, reboot loops, and GPS failures are all reported.
Test Sensus extensively — CarPlay, navigation, climate control, all audio sources. Any reboot during test = known issue.
Est. cost: $0 software update / $1,500–$3,500 head unit Source: Volvo Sensus complaints

Suspension & Air Ride

Air suspension (Four-C / standard) wear

High Attention
Air springs and compressor on XC90 air ride fail at 80k–120k miles.
Check ride height level side-to-side and front-to-rear.
Est. cost: $600–$1,200 per air spring / $800–$1,500 compressor Source: XC90 air suspension pattern

AWD

Haldex AWD coupling fluid

Monitor
Annual Haldex fluid service is required. Vibration at highway speed = neglected.
Test highway cornering.
Est. cost: $150–$300 Haldex service Source: Haldex AWD standard

Documentation

Volvo specialist PPI — mandatory

Critical
Any used Volvo XC90 requires a specialist PPI with Volvo VIDA/DiCE diagnostic scan.
Budget $150–$200 for PPI. Non-negotiable.
Est. cost: $150–$200 PPI / unknown deferred costs without Source: Volvo ownership standard
