2016 Kia Sorento
LX / EX / SX / SX Limited (UM, 3rd gen, 2016-2020)
Watchful

3rd-gen Sorento is a well-rounded family SUV. The 2.4L Nu and 2.0T engines carry KSDS recall exposure. The 3.3L Lambda V6 avoids the bearing issue entirely and is the most reliable choice. AWD coupling fluid service is the most commonly missed maintenance item. Strong value when recall history and AWD service are confirmed.

Top concern
Nu/2.0T engine bearing recall / AWD coupling

Powertrain

Nu / 2.0T engine bearing recall (KSDS)

High Attention
Multiple Kia KSDS campaigns apply. Lambda V6 is exempt — buyers should prefer V6 for lower risk.
Run VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. Prefer V6 if concerned about engine risk.
Est. cost: $0 under recall / $5,500–$8,000 engine Source: Kia KSDS recall family

3.3L Lambda V6 — low risk powertrain

Low Risk
Lambda V6 runs reliably past 200k with synthetic oil. No KSDS exposure.
Verify synthetic oil change records.
Est. cost: Maintenance only Source: Lambda V6 track record

AWD System

AWD coupling fluid — commonly skipped

High Attention
Rear coupling requires fluid every 30k miles. Neglect causes shudder on cornering and eventual failure.
Test highway-speed cornering for vibration or shudder.
Est. cost: $150–$300 fluid / $800–$1,600 coupling replacement Source: AWD maintenance requirement

Transmission

6AT automatic fluid condition

Monitor
Fluid change every 60k. Dark fluid = overdue.
Check fluid color and smell.
Est. cost: $200–$350 flush Source: Kia service interval

Electrical

UVO infotainment connectivity

Low Risk
UVO connectivity issues; software updates available.
Test all infotainment and CarPlay/Android Auto.
Est. cost: $0 update Source: Kia campaigns

Documentation

Engine recall + AWD service proof

High Attention
Both engine recall completion and AWD fluid service history are required documents.
Run nhtsa.gov/recalls + request service records including AWD fluid change.
Est. cost: Walk away from unresolved engine campaigns Source: NHTSA

NHTSA Recalls (verify completion via VIN)

Multiple
Engine recalls — VIN check
Kia Nu/Theta engines have multiple engine-failure/KSDS campaigns by year and trim. Run the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls and confirm all engine recalls are closed.
Multiple
Open recalls — VIN check
Campaigns vary by build date. Run the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls and require every open recall closed before purchase.
