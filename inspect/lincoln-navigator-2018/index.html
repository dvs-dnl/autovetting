2018 Lincoln Navigator
Standard / Reserve / Black Label (U553, 2018-2024)
Watchful

The 4th-gen Navigator shares the F-150/Expedition platform with a premium Lincoln interior and air suspension standard. The 3.5T EcoBoost cam phasers and 10-speed shudder are the same concerns as the Expedition. Air suspension repair is a significant additional cost. Outstanding luxury full-size value versus European competition.

Powertrain

3.5L EcoBoost cam phaser noise

Monitor
Same cam phaser rattle as F-150/Expedition above 60k miles.
Cold-start rattle check first 5 seconds.
Est. cost: $1,200–$3,000 cam phaser replacement Source: Ford 3.5T pattern

10-speed 10R80 shudder

High Attention
TSB shudder at 25–45 mph under light throttle.
Test extensively at light throttle 25–45 mph.
Est. cost: $350–$550 fluid + calibration / $4,500+ transmission Source: Ford TSB

Air Suspension

Air suspension airbag and compressor

High Attention
Standard air suspension bags and compressor fail at 80k–120k miles.
Check ride height level. Listen for compressor cycling at idle.
Est. cost: $600–$1,500 per bag / $800–$1,800 compressor Source: Full-size SUV air suspension pattern

Electrical

SYNC 3 and Lincoln-specific features

Low Risk
SYNC 3 is reliable. Test all luxury features — massage, panoramic, ambient lighting.
Test all features.
Est. cost: $0 update Source: Ford/Lincoln campaigns

Body

Aluminium body panel damage cost

Monitor
Aluminium panels are expensive to repair after collision.
Inspect all panels for repair evidence. Run CARFAX.
Est. cost: Aluminium repair 30–50% premium over steel Source: Insurance data

Documentation

Recall check + air suspension service history

High Attention
Three recalls possible. Air suspension service history is a key document.
Run nhtsa.gov/recalls.
Est. cost: Walk away from unresolved critical recalls Source: NHTSA
