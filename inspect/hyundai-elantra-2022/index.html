What the VIN tells you

The 7th-gen Elantra (CN7, 2021–present) is sold with four very different powertrains, and trim is the most reliable decode. SE/SEL/Limited use the 2.0L Smartstream MPI Atkinson four (147 hp) on a CVT (Hyundai's IVT) — the simple, low-risk choice, and notably NOT a Theta II engine, so it has no engine-fire-recall exposure. N Line uses the 1.6L turbo GDI four (201 hp) with a 7-speed dual-clutch; the Elantra N uses the 2.0L turbo GDI four (276 hp) with a 6-speed manual or 8-speed wet DCT; and the Hybrid pairs a 1.6L GDI four with an electric motor for 139 hp combined through a 6-speed DCT. The 8th VIN digit encodes the engine (the 2.0T N is commonly reported as 'K' and the 1.6 hybrid as 'J'), but confirm the engine for your specific VIN at autovetting.com/decode or vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov rather than trusting trim badging alone on a used car. Run the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls before purchase.
