2018 Audi A4
Premium / Premium Plus / Prestige (B9, 2017-2025)
High Attention

The B9 Audi A4 is a genuinely excellent executive sedan — refined, comfortable, and well-built. But like all German luxury cars at this price point, maintenance costs are significantly above average. DSG fluid service, carbon walnut blasting on the EA888, timing chain service at 80k+ miles, and coolant flange cracks are all known line items. Audi-specialist PPI is mandatory. Budget $2,000–$4,000/year honestly.

Powertrain

EA888 timing chain tensioner

High Attention
EA888 gen 3 timing chain tensioner can fail at 60k–100k miles if oil changes were extended. Startup rattle is the warning sign.
Cold-start and listen for a rattle in the first 5 seconds.
Est. cost: $1,200–$2,500 timing chain service Source: EA888 gen3 known pattern

GDI carbon buildup on intake valves

Monitor
Port injection was added on gen3 EA888 to reduce carbon — but older B9 builds still accumulate deposits.
Test idle smoothness and cold-start behavior.
Est. cost: $400–$800 walnut blast cleaning Source: GDI carbon pattern

Coolant flange (plastic) failure

High Attention
The plastic coolant flange on the B9 A4 cracks and causes rapid coolant loss — a well-known VW/Audi EA888 failure mode.
Inspect coolant level cold before driving. Check for any coolant smell in engine bay.
Est. cost: $300–$600 coolant flange + flush Source: Audi EA888 documented issue

Transmission

7-speed S tronic DSG fluid service

High Attention
S tronic (DQ500 or DL382) requires mechatronic fluid every 40k miles. Shudder at low speed = overdue service.
Test low-speed stop-and-go for shudder or jerk.
Est. cost: $400–$700 DSG service / $2,000–$4,000 mechatronic Source: Audi DSG service standard

Electronics

MMI / Virtual Cockpit faults

Monitor
Audi MMI and Virtual Cockpit accumulate fault codes. Scan with VCDS before purchase.
Have car scanned with VCDS at an Audi specialist.
Est. cost: $150–$200 PPI scan / $300–$2,000+ module faults Source: Pre-purchase Audi scan standard

Suspension

Air suspension or CDDS faults (equipped models)

Monitor
Air suspension and electronic damper faults appear on higher-spec B9 A4s.
Test ride quality over rough pavement and check for any fault lights.
Est. cost: $400–$2,500 per corner depending on component Source: Common Audi suspension

Documentation

Audi specialist PPI — non-negotiable

Critical
Any used Audi requires a specialist PPI with VCDS diagnostic scan.
Budget $150–$200 for PPI. Walk away from any seller who refuses.
Est. cost: $150–$200 PPI / unknown deferred costs without it Source: Audi ownership standard
