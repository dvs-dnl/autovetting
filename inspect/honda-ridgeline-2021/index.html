Powertrain

3.5L VCM V6 — motor mount wear

Monitor
VCM (Variable Cylinder Management) activates in fuel-saving 3-cylinder mode, causing vibration that wears motor mounts prematurely. Replacement mounts or VCM disabler ($60) prevent recurrence.
At warm idle in Drive, feel for vibration through steering wheel and shifter. Any shudder = motor mounts likely worn.
Est. cost: $200–$600 motor mounts; $60 VCM Muzzler (preventive) Source: Honda VCM mount pattern

9-speed automatic — smooth and reliable

Low Risk
Honda 9AT is well-sorted. No widespread issues. Change fluid every 30k for towing applications.
Test all gears including tow mode. Smooth shifts expected.
Est. cost: $150–$250 fluid service Source: Honda 9AT reliability

i-VTM4 AWD

Rear differential fluid — critical service

High Risk without
The i-VTM4 rear diff requires DPS-F fluid every 15k–30k miles. Neglected fluid leads to chatter and bearing failure. Check service records specifically for this item.
Request differential fluid service records. Tight turn test: no vibration or binding expected.
Est. cost: $150–$300 diff fluid service; $1,500–$3,000 diff repair if neglected Source: Honda i-VTM4 service requirement

What the VIN tells you

Unlike most trucks in its class, the 2nd-generation Ridgeline (2017–2023) offers only one engine — the 3.5L J35Y6 i-VTEC V6 (280 hp) with a 9-speed automatic — so there is no engine family to decode. Two facts matter more for a 2021 specifically. First, i-VTM4 torque-vectoring all-wheel drive became standard on every trim for 2021 (the front-drive base configuration sold in 2020 was dropped), so a genuine 2021 should be AWD — verify the drivetrain with a full VIN decode at vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov and treat any “FWD 2021 Ridgeline” listing as a red flag for a mistitled vehicle. Second, position 10 of the VIN encodes the model year (M = 2021); confirm it matches the title, then run the full VIN at nhtsa.gov to verify the open-recall history is closed before purchase. Because all 2021 Ridgelines share the same V6, the inspection priorities are identical regardless of trim: VCM motor-mount vibration at warm idle and i-VTM4 rear-differential fluid-service history.
