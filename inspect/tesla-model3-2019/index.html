Battery & Range

HV battery health and range degradation

High Attention
Battery capacity degrades with age and charge cycles. Compare current range reading to EPA rating — more than 15% loss on a 3-year-old car is above average degradation.
Charge to 90% and check range estimate. Compare to EPA rating. Any degradation over 15% = negotiate or walk away.
Est. cost: $8,000–$15,000 HV battery replacement if severe Source: Tesla battery degradation data

Charging port and onboard charger

Monitor
Charging port latch, NACS/CCS adapter compatibility, and onboard charger faults are reported.
Test charging with a Level 2 charger before purchase.
Est. cost: $400–$1,500 charging port Source: Tesla service pattern

Drive Unit

Rear motor bearing noise (pre-2020 LR AWD)

High Attention
Pre-2020 Dual Motor Long Range cars had rear motor bearing whine. Tesla revised the design. Listen for a whine that increases with speed.
Highway speed listen test — any whine proportional to speed = rear motor concern.
Est. cost: $2,500–$5,000 drive unit Source: Tesla LR AWD known issue

Body Quality

Panel gaps and paint quality

Monitor
Early Model 3 production had larger-than-average panel gaps. Mostly cosmetic but indicates QC variation.
Inspect all panel gaps — hood, trunk, doors. Any gap larger than 5mm is above average.
Est. cost: $0–$500 adjustment / cosmetic issue only Source: Consumer reports

Interior

Glass roof seals and wind noise

Monitor
Glass roof seals degrade causing wind noise at highway speed.
Test at 70 mph for wind noise from roof edges.
Est. cost: $150–$400 seal replacement Source: Common Tesla complaint

Documentation

Battery health report + recall check

High Attention
Tesla publishes battery health data on service app. Four recalls possible including Autopilot-related campaigns.
Request service history from Tesla app. Run nhtsa.gov/recalls.
Est. cost: Walk away from >20% battery degradation without price adjustment Source: NHTSA

What the VIN tells you

The 1st-gen Model 3 (2017+) came in three drivetrain configurations and the inspection priorities differ significantly. Standard Range Plus / Standard Range (2019 RWD, 283hp) — single rear motor. Long Range AWD (346hp) — dual motor, front-induction + rear-permanent-magnet. Performance (450hp) — dual motor with larger inverters + larger brakes. Battery chemistry changed across years: 2019 Standard Range Plus used NCA chemistry; LFP came in 2021+ for standard-range variants. Tell variants apart fastest from the badge on the trunk lid and the build sheet (accessible via the touchscreen: Service → Vehicle Information). Critical inspections that apply to all 2019 Model 3s: rear inverter coolant pump (a documented failure pattern at 40-70k miles), Boot/door rubber seal water-ingress at the trunk, and the MCU2 infotainment computer (replaced under recall on most 2018-2020 cars, but verify completion). Drivetrain-specific: Performance models often have track use history — inspect tire wear, brake wear, and check Autopilot computer revision (HW2.5 vs HW3.0; HW3.0 is the floor for Full Self-Driving).
