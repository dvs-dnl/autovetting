2017 Nissan Sentra
S / SV / SR / SL (B17, 7th gen, 2013-2019)
Watchful

B17 Sentra is among the most affordable used compact sedans available. The 1.8L engine itself is reasonably reliable — the Xtronic CVT is the buyer risk. CVT failure mirrors the Altima pattern when NS-3 fluid was not changed every 30k miles. Service history is everything on any CVT Nissan.

Top concern
Xtronic CVT fluid and reliability

Powertrain

Xtronic CVT fluid and reliability

High Attention
Nissan CVTs require NS-3 fluid every 30k miles. Neglected service leads to belt slip, shudder, and failure.
Ask for CVT fluid service history. Test highway acceleration — any shudder or hunting = CVT wear.
Est. cost: $200–$350 fluid service / $3,000–$4,500 CVT replacement Source: Nissan CVT maintenance requirement

Engine oil consumption

Monitor
MRA8 can consume oil above 100k miles with extended service intervals.
Pull dipstick before driving.
Est. cost: Monitor / $0 if caught early Source: Consumer reports

Suspension

Front strut and bearing wear

Monitor
Common at 80k+ miles. Hum that changes with steering input = bearing.
Highway noise check; bounce each front corner.
Est. cost: $300–$600 per strut assembly Source: Common wear

Electrical

NissanConnect infotainment

Low Risk
Known slow-boot and freeze issues. Software updates resolve most.
Test all infotainment functions.
Est. cost: $0 update Source: Nissan campaigns

Body

Paint quality and rust

Low Risk
B17 paint chips easily. Salt-belt rockers can rust early.
Inspect rocker panels.
Est. cost: $0–$500 treatment Source: Consumer pattern

Documentation

CVT fluid history + recall check

High Attention
CVT fluid service history is the most critical document on any Sentra.
Run nhtsa.gov/recalls. Request all maintenance records.
Est. cost: Walk away from CVT without fluid history Source: NHTSA

NHTSA Recalls (verify completion via VIN)

17V-253
Passenger Airbag Inflator (Non-Deploy)
Manufacturing error in the inflator — passenger frontal airbag may not deploy in a crash. Covers 2014, 2016 and 2017 Sentra. Dealer replaces the inflator free.
18V-551
Ignition Switch Rotation (Mechanical Key)
Key-ring weight can rotate the ignition out of RUN and shut off the engine while driving. 2017 Sentra with mechanical-key ignition. Dealer inspects and replaces the switch.
21V-135
Brake Light Switch Contamination
Switch contamination can keep brake lights from illuminating — 2016-2019 Sentra, Nissan campaign PM971. Dealer replaces the switch and adds a protective grommet.

Open recalls — VIN check
Campaigns vary by build date. Run the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls and require every open recall closed before purchase.
