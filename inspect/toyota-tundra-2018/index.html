What the VIN tells you

The 2nd-gen Tundra (2014–2021) came with two V8s and no other powertrain. 5.7L 3UR-FE V8 (381hp) is the mainstream engine, found on roughly 90% of trucks — SR5, Limited, Platinum, 1794, TRD Pro. 4.6L 1UR-FE V8 (310hp) was the base SR trim only and is uncommon. Both engines are exceptionally reliable; neither has a chronic failure pattern in this generation. The differences that matter to a buyer: the 5.7L tows ~10,200 lbs vs the 4.6L's ~6,400 lbs, fuel economy is ~1 mpg different, and finding parts for the 4.6L can be slightly slower because the volume is lower. Confirm engine displacement on the door-jamb sticker (it lists "Engine Displacement: 5.7L" or "4.6L") or by the trim badge on the tailgate.
