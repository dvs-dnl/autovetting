2017 Hyundai Tucson
SE / Eco / Sport / Limited / Ultimate (TL, 3rd gen, 2016-2021)
Watchful

The TL Tucson is a competent compact SUV with good road manners — but the 2.0L Nu engine has a documented history of bearing failure and engine seizure (the Theta II successor shares some characteristics). Hyundai's broad engine recall campaigns cover select VINs. The 1.6T is the more engaging choice but requires turbo oil-feed line inspection. Confirm knock sensor monitoring (KSDS) recall coverage before purchasing any Tucson in this generation.

Powertrain

2.0L Nu engine bearing / seizure risk

High Attention
Hyundai's KSDS (Knock Sensor Detection System) recall campaign covers Nu and Theta II engines with bearing wear that can lead to engine seizure. Multiple engine campaigns apply to specific production ranges — verify by VIN.
Run the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm all engine recall campaigns are complete. Listen for a metallic knock at idle — not to be confused with normal injector tick.
Est. cost: $0 under recall / $5,500–$8,500 engine replacement if seizure occurs Source: NHTSA recall database + Hyundai KSDS campaign

1.6T turbo oil feed line inspection

Monitor
The 1.6 Gamma turbo oil-feed banjo bolt can develop small leaks at the cylinder head junction at 80k+ miles.
Inspect the banjo bolt fitting on the turbo oil line for seepage. Any leak = address before purchase.
Est. cost: $150–$400 banjo bolt + gaskets Source: 1.6 Gamma service pattern

DCT / automatic transmission fluid

Monitor
The Eco 1.6T uses a 7-speed DCT that requires fluid service at 40k miles — often skipped. DCT shudder under 15 mph is the symptom.
Test low-speed crawl in stop-and-go traffic. Any shudder or jerkiness at under 15 mph = DCT service needed.
Est. cost: $250–$450 DCT fluid service / $3,500–$5,500 clutch pack Source: Hyundai DCT service pattern

Electrical

Infotainment and touchscreen

Low Risk
AVN system has known responsiveness issues. Verify all functions including Android Auto/Apple CarPlay compatibility.
Test infotainment fully.

Suspension

Rear trailing arm bushing wear

Monitor
Rear trailing arm bushings wear at 80k–100k miles on TL Tucson, causing vague handling and tire wear.
Check rear tire wear pattern — uneven inner wear indicates trailing arm bushing wear or alignment loss.
Est. cost: $300–$600 bushing replacement Source: Common wear pattern

Body

Paint quality

Low Risk
TL Tucson has softer-than-average paint that chips and scratches easily.
Inspect hood leading edge and door handles for paint chips.
Est. cost: $0–$500 touch-up or wrap Source: Consumer reports

Documentation

Engine recall completion (multiple campaigns)

Critical
Multiple Hyundai engine recall campaigns apply to this VIN range. Non-completion = potential engine seizure risk.
Run VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. All Hyundai KSDS and engine bearing recall campaigns must show complete.
Est. cost: Walk away if critical engine recalls are unresolved Source: NHTSA
