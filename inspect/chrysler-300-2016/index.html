5.7L HEMI MDS lifter failure

High Risk on HEMI at 80k+
MDS cylinder deactivation causes accelerated lifter wear. Symptoms: persistent tick at warm idle.
Warm engine completely. Listen at idle for tick. Ask if MDS has been disabled.

Recall

Open recalls — check VIN (Takata scope stops at the 2015 car)

Monitor
FCA's active airbag-inflator campaign list runs 2005–2015 for the Chrysler 300. A 2016 is one model year outside it. This is worth knowing in both directions: if you are cross-shopping an older 300, a 2015-or-earlier car with an unrepaired inflator is under FCA's stop-drive advisory and should not be driven home.
Run the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls before you go and see the car. Any campaign that comes back open = do not purchase until the dealer has closed it. Recall work is always free, and completion is logged against the VIN, so a seller's word is not evidence.
Est. cost: Free under recall Source: FCA Takata affected-model list (mopar.com), verified 2026-08-09

NHTSA Recalls (verify completion via VIN)

VIN check
Severity: Informational
FCA's airbag-inflator campaign covers the 2005–2015 Chrysler 300; a 2016 is outside it. Run the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls for the campaigns that do apply — and note that an unrepaired 2015-or-earlier car is under FCA's stop-drive advisory. Remedy: Any open campaign is repaired free by a dealer.

What the VIN tells you

The 2nd-gen 300 (LX, 2011-2023) came with two V engines that share an LX platform but inspect very differently. 3.6L Pentastar V6 (292hp, 8-speed automatic, RWD or AWD) — fitted to base 300 Limited, 300 Touring, 300S V6. The Pentastar is one of FCA's most-proven engines but has a documented oil-cooler housing leak pattern (a known $500-800 service at 80-120k miles) and a rocker-arm-bearing issue on 2011-2013 cars largely resolved by 2016. 5.7L HEMI V8 (363hp, 8-speed automatic, RWD only) — fitted to 300S V8 and 300C variants. The HEMI carries the same MDS lifter failure pattern as the Ram 1500 and Charger/Challenger: lifters can fail at 60-120k miles on neglected oil schedules ($3,000-6,500 repair). Service record showing 5,000-mile or shorter oil intervals is the buyer's protection. Both engines pair with the ZF 8HP70 8-speed, which is exceptionally reliable. Tell engines apart by the trunk badge ('V8' or 'HEMI' is explicit) and exhaust outlet count (single vs dual). Run the VIN for open recalls regardless of engine — the Takata inflator campaign covers the 2005–2015 300, not this one. Full VIN decode at autovetting.com/inspect.
