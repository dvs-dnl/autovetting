2018 Lexus IS
IS200t / IS300 / IS350 / IS350 AWD (3rd gen XE30, 2014–2020)
Low Risk

The 3rd-gen IS is a sharp-driving, highly reliable sport sedan. The IS350 with the 3.5L V6 is the standout — naturally aspirated, smooth, and proven. The IS200t/IS300 2.0T is also reliable but less satisfying. Main buyer risks are the same as any sport sedan: modified suspension, aggressive driving history, and GDI carbon on the direct-injection V6. Lexus’s Takata scope ends with the 2013 IS 250/350, so this car is not part of it; well-maintained examples hit 200k miles without drama.

Lexus lists the IS 250/350 for 2006–2013, the IS 250C/350C convertible for 2010–2015 and the IS-F for 2008–2014 as Takata-affected. A 2018 sedan is outside all three. “IS models through 2019” is not a scope that exists.

NHTSA Recalls (verify completion via VIN)

VIN check
Severity: Informational
Lexus lists the 2006–2013 IS 250/350, the 2010–2015 IS C and the 2008–2014 IS-F as Takata-affected. A 2018 sedan is outside all three. Run the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls for the campaigns that do apply. Remedy: Any open campaign is repaired free by a dealer.

What the VIN tells you

The 2018 IS came three ways and the badge tells you the powertrain. IS300 (rear-drive) = 2.0L turbo four (8AR-FTS, 241hp) with 8-speed automatic; the VIN engine character is 'A' on this car. IS300 AWD and IS350 both use the 3.5L V6 (2GR-FKS); the VIN engine character is 'Z'. The AWD-only IS300 detunes that V6 to 260hp with a 6-speed automatic, while the IS350 makes 311hp (8-speed RWD / 6-speed AWD). Note: the checklist's '2GR-FSE, 260hp' label describes the 2014-2015 IS350; for 2018 every V6 IS is the newer 2GR-FKS. Buyer priorities differ by engine: on the turbo four, check for oil consumption and a healthy wastegate/turbo; on the V6, budget for a GDI walnut-blast decarbon at 80-100k miles. All trims: run the VIN for open recalls before purchase — but note the Takata inflator campaign covers the 2006–2013 IS, not this one.
