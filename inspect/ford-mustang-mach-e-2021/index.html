The Mach-E is Ford's flagship EV — crossover styling, spacious interior, and multiple range/performance variants. Early production had a high-voltage battery contactor recall that must be confirmed closed. The frunk (front trunk) door latch had a recall on some units. Ford's OTA update cadence is active. 12V battery is a common failure point. Overall a capable and improving EV platform.

Recalls — Check First

HV battery main contactor recall (22V-412)

Critical — Check VIN
Ford recalled 2021–2022 Mach-E units for high-voltage battery main contactors that can overheat and cause a loss of drive power.
Run VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 22V-412 must be confirmed closed.
Est. cost: Free dealer repair (software update / contactor) Source: NHTSA recall 22V-412

Frunk latch — open-recall check

High Risk — Check VIN
Front trunk (frunk) latch can open unexpectedly while driving on some units.
Run VIN at nhtsa.gov. Test frunk latch engagement manually.
Est. cost: Free dealer repair where a campaign applies Source: NHTSA recalls (VIN lookup)

12V battery failure

High Risk
Mach-E 12V battery fails and strands the vehicle — same pattern as other EVs.
Ask if 12V has been replaced. Check for any low-voltage warning history.
Est. cost: $200–$400 12V replacement Source: Mach-E 12V pattern
